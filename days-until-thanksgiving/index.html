Questions

How many days until Thanksgiving?

The countdown at the top of this page shows the exact number of days, hours and minutes until Thanksgiving in your own time zone. Thanksgiving 2026 is Thursday, November 26.

What date is Thanksgiving 2026?

Thanksgiving 2026 in the United States is Thursday, November 26. It always falls on the fourth Thursday of November, so the date moves between November 22 and 28 each year.

Why is Thanksgiving always on a Thursday?

Presidents set Thanksgiving on a Thursday by proclamation from the 1860s on, and a 1941 law fixed it as the fourth Thursday of November, which is the rule the United States still follows.

When is Thanksgiving 2027?

Thanksgiving 2027 is Thursday, November 25.

When is Canadian Thanksgiving?

Canada celebrates Thanksgiving on the second Monday of October, so it comes about six weeks before Thanksgiving in the United States.

What national days are in Thanksgiving week?

The list on this page shows every verified observance from November 1 to Thanksgiving, such as National Carbonated Beverage with Caffeine Day, World Toilet Day, Universal Childrens Day. The CelebriDay app names each one in a morning notification.
