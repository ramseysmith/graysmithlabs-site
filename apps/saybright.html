Under the hood

How we built it.

Designed, engineered, and launched end to end by Graysmith Labs. Everything here is available to client projects too. Work with us on an app of your own.

  • Native WidgetKit. A native Swift widget extension that shares data with the app through an App Group.
  • Private recordings. Voice recordings are captured and stored on device only, never uploaded.
  • Expo Router. File based navigation on Expo SDK 54 and React Native 0.81, in TypeScript.
  • Launch ready. RevenueCat subscriptions, AdMob with App Tracking Transparency, and full App Store metadata.

FAQ

SayBright, answered.

SayBright is an iPhone affirmations app with 340 affirmations in ten categories such as confidence, gratitude and self love. The free plan gives five affirmations a day, the whole Confidence category and a preview of every other category, plus a daily reminder, streak milestones and your own written affirmations. Premium adds every category with no daily limit, home and lock screen widgets, recordings in your own voice and no ads.

Is SayBright free?

Yes. The free plan gives five affirmations a day, the whole Confidence category, a preview of every other category, streaks, reminders and your own written affirmations. Premium is $0.99 a month or $9.99 a year with a free week first for eligible new subscribers, and opens every category with no daily limit, widgets, voice recordings and no ads.

Can I put affirmations on my lock screen?

Yes, with Premium. SayBright has home screen and lock screen widgets that refresh through the day.

Can I write my own affirmations?

Yes, free. Write your own in My Affirmations. With Premium you can also record affirmations in your own voice, and recordings never leave your phone.

How do streaks work?

Open your daily affirmation each day to build a streak, with celebrations at 7, 14, 30, 60, 90, 180 and 365 days.

How many affirmations does SayBright have?

340 affirmations in ten categories: Confidence, Gratitude, Morning Energy, Self Love, Career Growth, Relationships, Health and Body, Wealth and Abundance, Calm and Anxiety Relief, and Creativity.
