SayBright

A daily affirmation practice that sticks.

SayBright greets you each morning with an affirmation from the categories you care about most. Build a streak, save favorites, record affirmations in your own voice, and keep them on your home screen.

What it does

Features

340 plus affirmations

Ten categories including confidence, gratitude, self love, and morning energy.

Streak milestones

Celebrations at 7, 14, 30, 60, 90, 180, and 365 days to keep the habit going.

Widgets

Home screen and lock screen widgets that refresh throughout the day.

Your own voice

Record an affirmation and hear it played back during your practice. Recordings stay on the phone.

Share cards

Turn any affirmation into a polished card for Instagram Stories or iMessage.

Daily reminders

Pick a time and SayBright comes to you.

Under the hood

How we built it.

Designed, engineered, and launched end to end by Graysmith Labs. Everything here is available to client projects too.

  • Native WidgetKit. A native Swift widget extension that shares data with the app through an App Group.
  • Private recordings. Voice recordings are captured and stored on device only, never uploaded.
  • Expo Router. File based navigation on Expo SDK 54 and React Native 0.81, in TypeScript.
  • Launch ready. RevenueCat subscriptions, AdMob with App Tracking Transparency, and full App Store metadata.
