Under the hood

How we built it.

Designed, engineered, and launched end to end by Graysmith Labs. Everything here is available to client projects too. Work with us on an app of your own.

  • Native WidgetKit. A native Swift widget extension that shares data with the app through an App Group.
  • Private recordings. Voice recordings are captured and stored on device only, never uploaded.
  • Expo Router. File based navigation on Expo SDK 54 and React Native 0.81, in TypeScript.
  • Launch ready. RevenueCat subscriptions, AdMob with App Tracking Transparency, and full App Store metadata.

FAQ

SayBright, answered.

SayBright is a daily affirmations app for iPhone that helps you start and keep a daily affirmation habit: five free affirmations a day picked from the topics you choose, free widgets, a Lock Screen sunrise that rises as you say them, streaks, and a free seasonal pack each month it runs, from 950 affirmations that all start with I am, in twenty categories such as confidence, courage, sleep and rest, and self love. No subscription is needed for the free plan. Premium ($0.99 a month or $9.99 a year) adds every category with no daily limit, widgets that change all day, affirmations recorded in your own voice, a weekly streak save and no ads.

Is SayBright free?

Yes. The free plan gives five affirmations a day, the whole Confidence category, a preview of every other category, the Lock Screen Sunrise, widgets, streaks, reminders and your own written affirmations. Premium is $0.99 a month or $9.99 a year with a free week first for eligible new subscribers, and opens every category with no daily limit, widgets that change all day, voice recordings and no ads.

Can I put affirmations on my lock screen?

Yes, free. SayBright has free Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets with today's affirmation, plus the Sunrise: today's five affirmations on your Lock Screen, where each one you say lifts the sun. With Premium the widgets change all day.

Can I write my own affirmations?

Yes, free. Write your own in My Affirmations. With Premium you can also record affirmations in your own voice, and recordings never leave your phone.

How do I start a daily affirmation habit?

Pick a fixed moment, such as right after you wake up, and say three to five affirmations out loud at that time every day. Choose ones you can believe, keep them in the present tense, and use a reminder and a streak so a missed day is easy to notice. SayBright does this for free: a daily reminder at a time you pick, five affirmations a day and streak milestones from 7 to 365 days.

How do streaks work?

Open your daily affirmation each day to build a streak, with celebrations at 7, 14, 30, 60, 90, 180 and 365 days.

Is there an affirmation app where I can record my own voice?

Yes. SayBright Premium lets you record any affirmation in your own voice and play it back; recordings stay on your phone. Writing your own affirmations is free.

How many affirmations does SayBright have?

950 affirmations in twenty categories, every one starting with I am: Confidence, Courage, Joy and Play, Self Love, Morning Energy, Career Growth, Wealth and Abundance, Focus, Habits and Discipline, Students and Exams, Creativity, New Beginnings, Relationships, Gratitude, Parenthood, Boundaries, Calm and Anxiety Relief, Sleep and Rest, Healing and Letting Go, and Health and Body.

Is there an affirmation app that does not require a subscription?

SayBright's free plan needs no subscription: five affirmations a day, the full Confidence category, widgets, streaks, reminders and your own written affirmations stay free. A subscription only adds the other categories in full, widgets that change all day, voice recordings and removes ads.

How do I put affirmations on my iPhone lock screen?

Free: add SayBright's Lock Screen widget for today's affirmation, or make a wallpaper with your affirmation and set it as your Lock Screen. With SayBright Premium the widget changes through the day. Step by step instructions are in the lock screen guide on graysmithlabs.com.
