BlitzTap Privacy Policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

BlitzTap is a reaction time game made by Graysmith Labs LLC. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: there is no account, and your scores and settings stay on your phone.

What stays on your device

Your high scores, coins, statistics, skins, achievements and settings (sound, haptics and reminders) are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive this data, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Reminders

The daily streak reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. It is never sent through our servers, and you can turn it off in Settings.

Purchases

The one time Remove Ads purchase is processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to confirm whether you own it. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase receipt and basic device information such as the app version and country. It does not receive your name, email or Apple ID. You can read the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Advertising

BlitzTap shows ads through Google AdMob unless you buy Remove Ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers, including the Advertising Identifier when you allow it, ad interactions such as impressions and taps, and general device information to serve and measure ads. Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences. You can read the Google privacy policy.

Tracking permission

BlitzTap asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before AdMob may use your Advertising Identifier for personalized ads. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalized. You can change this at any time in the Settings app under Privacy and Security, then Tracking.

Usage analytics

Starting with a future version, BlitzTap may send a small number of anonymous usage events, such as finishing onboarding, finishing a game, setting a new personal best, turning on reminders and opening the Remove Ads purchase, so we can tell which parts of the game people enjoy. These events carry counts and labels only, never your scores, name, email or advertising identifier. They are tied to a random identifier created on your device, are processed by PostHog, and are not used for advertising or sold. You can read the PostHog privacy policy.

Diagnostics

AdMob and RevenueCat may collect anonymous crash and performance data to keep their services reliable.

Children

BlitzTap is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. There are no accounts, and ads are limited to general audience content.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling BlitzTap deletes everything it stored. To reset what AdMob holds, reset your Advertising Identifier in the Settings app under Privacy and Security, then Apple Advertising. To remove purchase records held by RevenueCat, email us and we will request the deletion for you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
