Drift

White noise and sleep sounds, one tap away.

Drift helps people fall asleep faster and stay asleep longer. Nine carefully mixed soundscapes, a sleep timer, and a pitch black interface that stays out of the way at 2am.

What it does

Features

Nine soundscapes

Rain, ocean waves, thunderstorm, white noise, brown noise, fan, forest, fireplace, and wind.

Sleep timer

Fade out after 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, or 2 hours, so audio does not run all night.

Plays with the screen locked

Background audio keeps going when the phone sleeps, with volume right on the main screen.

Works offline

Every sound ships inside the app. No streaming, no buffering, no signal needed.

Built for the dark

A minimal black interface that will not light up the room or wake a partner.

Premium

A simple monthly subscription removes ads and supports ongoing development.

Under the hood

How we built it.

Designed, engineered, and launched end to end by Graysmith Labs. Everything here is available to client projects too.

  • Seamless background audio. iOS background audio mode with loops tuned so there is no audible gap or click at the seam.
  • Expo and React Native. Expo SDK 54, React Native 0.81, and Reanimated 4, written in strict TypeScript.
  • Subscriptions and ads. RevenueCat for the Premium subscription and Google AdMob for the free tier.
  • Shipped and iterated. Released in February 2026 and updated steadily since, now on version 1.16.
