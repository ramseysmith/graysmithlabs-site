FAQ

Drift, answered.

Drift is a free iPhone and iPad app that plays seamless sleep sounds (white noise, rain, ocean, stream and fan free; thunder, forest, night, fire, wind and cafe with Premium) with a sleep timer that fades out, playback with the screen locked, and no internet needed.

Is Drift free?

Yes. White noise, rain, ocean, stream and fan are free. Premium unlocks six more sounds and removes ads, and new subscribers get a free week.

Does Drift work without internet?

Yes. Every sound is stored inside the app, so it plays on a plane, in a cabin or with your phone in airplane mode.

Will Drift keep playing when my screen locks?

Yes. Drift uses background audio, so sound keeps going with the phone locked, all night or until the sleep timer fades it out.

Which sound is best for sleep?

Steady sounds like white noise and fan are best for masking sharp noises such as snoring or traffic. Rain, ocean and stream suit people who find pure noise harsh. Drift lets you preview every sound, so try a few for a night each.

Can I set a sleep timer?

Yes. Pick 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours and the sound fades out gently instead of stopping abruptly.
