Tallyhand

Say the job once. Get paid.

Tallyhand is an invoice, estimate and receipt maker for one person service businesses. Type or dictate what you did, like “Hendersons: mowed lawn 2 hrs at $40, mulch 3 bags at 6.50”, and it builds the line items, sends a clean PDF and tells you when someone is late.

What it does

Features

One sentence, one invoice

PDF in one tap

FAQ

Something else? Email us.

How does Quick add understand a sentence?

Start with the client and a colon if you like, then list each item with its price, separated by commas. “2 hrs at $40”, “3 bags @ 6.50”, “haul away 25” and “3 hours for $120” all work. It runs on your iPhone, and anything it cannot price is shown to you instead of guessed.

Can I speak the job instead of typing it?

Yes. Tap the microphone on your iPhone keyboard in Quick add and say it. Dictation is handled by your iPhone.

What kinds of businesses is Tallyhand for?

One person service businesses that bill per job or per hour: lawn care, house cleaning, handyman work, pet sitting, mobile detailing, tutoring and similar. If you need payroll, inventory or full bookkeeping, pair it with your accounting software and use the CSV export.

How do my clients pay?

Add payment instructions (Venmo, Zelle, checks) and a Pay online link from Square, PayPal, Stripe or Venmo in Settings. They print on every invoice and go in the message you send. Tallyhand does not handle the money itself.

Can Tallyhand make receipts?

Yes. Mark an invoice paid, choose how they paid, and send a receipt PDF with a Paid stamp.

Is Tallyhand free?

Your first 3 invoices or estimates are free with every feature. Premium, monthly or yearly with a free week, adds unlimited documents, your logo on every PDF with no Tallyhand line, and income reports with a CSV export for tax time.

How do I cancel or restore Premium?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Tallyhand. To restore a purchase on a new phone, open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. You can delete everything from Settings in the app. Read the privacy policy.
