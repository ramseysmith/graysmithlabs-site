CelebriDay

Every day is a holiday. Here is today's.

From National Pizza Day to World Emoji Day, there is something to celebrate every day of the year. CelebriDay delivers it each morning with a fun fact and a card made for sharing.

What it does

Features

Morning notifications

A cheerful alert with today's holiday and a fun fact before you are out of bed.

Color coded cards

Each holiday gets a vibrant card with a description, a fun fact, and a Share button.

The whole year

A full calendar with category dots, and a pull up sheet for any day you tap.

365 plus fun facts

A hand picked fact for every holiday, ready for dinner conversation.

Favorites

Heart the holidays you love to keep your own collection.

Your schedule

Premium lets you choose exactly when the daily alert arrives.

Under the hood

How we built it.

Designed, engineered, and launched end to end by Graysmith Labs. Everything here is available to client projects too.

  • Reliable daily notifications. Locally scheduled notifications, so the morning alert arrives without a server.
  • Share cards. Holiday cards render to images on device for sharing to Messages and social apps.
  • Current stack. Expo SDK 55, React Native 0.83, and Reanimated 4, kept on the latest releases.
  • Subscriptions. RevenueCat powers monthly and annual Premium, with AdMob for the free tier.
