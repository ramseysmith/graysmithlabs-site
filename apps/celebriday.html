FAQ

CelebriDay, answered.

CelebriDay is an iPhone and iPad app that tells you what national day it is today, every day of the year: a morning notification names today's holiday, and each of its 700 plus observances, from National Pizza Day to World Emoji Day, has a fun fact and a card to share on social media. Today and tomorrow are free in the app, and a free list of national days by month, with every date checked, is on the web at graysmithlabs.com/holidays/.

What national day is it today?

The today page at graysmithlabs.com/holidays/ lists every verified observance for today, and the CelebriDay app names it in a notification each morning, with a fun fact and a share card inside the app.

Are the holidays in CelebriDay real?

Every observance on the website is checked against a public source before it is published, and moving holidays are recalculated each year. The app uses the same list; its dates were checked in October 2026 and the few that were wrong were corrected.

Is CelebriDay free?

Yes. Today and tomorrow are free with ads, including the fun facts, sharing and the morning note at 8 AM. Premium, monthly or yearly with a free week to try it, opens the whole calendar and favorites, lets you pick when the note arrives and removes ads.

Can I use it to plan social media posts?

Yes. Every holiday has a card you can share to Instagram, Messages or anywhere else, and the month pages on graysmithlabs.com/holidays/ list every national day ahead for free.
