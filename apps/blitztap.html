BlitzTap

A reflex game that gets faster. Much faster.

A target appears. A grid of shapes appears below it. Tap the match before the clock runs out. BlitzTap is quick to learn and hard to put down, with four tiers that push reflexes and focus further every round.

What it does

Features

Four difficulty tiers

The grid grows, distractors multiply, and the timer shrinks until Tier 4 gives you about a second.

Streak multipliers

Five in a row for 2x, ten for 3x, twenty for 5x. One miss resets it all.

Feedback you can feel

A punchy pop and haptic on every hit, a screen shake and a hint on every miss.

Personal bests

High score, longest streak, top tier reached, and games played, all tracked.

Plays your way

Separate sound and haptic toggles, silent switch aware, and Reduce Motion support.

Fair and free

No energy systems, no wait timers, no pay to win. One purchase removes ads.

Under the hood

How we built it.

Designed, engineered, and launched end to end by Graysmith Labs. Everything here is available to client projects too.

  • Tuned game feel. Reanimated 4 animations, synced sound, and haptics so every tap lands with weight.
  • Accessible by default. Honors Reduce Motion and the silent switch, and mixes politely with your music.
  • Instant restart. Zero loading between rounds, so one more try is always one tap away.
  • Simple monetization. A single non consumable purchase through RevenueCat, with AdMob for the free tier.
