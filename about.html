About and contact

graysmithlabs.com is made by Graysmith Labs LLC, a small independent software studio in Colorado. We make iPhone apps (Drift is on Android too), build software for clients, and publish the free tools, holiday calendar and guides on this site.

Who makes it

Ramsey Graysmith founded the studio, runs it and is responsible for everything published here. The apps are built with React Native and Expo, and you can read more about hiring the studio on the services page.

How pages are made and checked

The guides explain the subjects our apps handle. Software assembles the holiday calendar and some page layouts from data we keep, and AI tools help with drafting, drawing and code. Only holiday pages with a written story are offered to search engines.

What is here

The free tools need no account. The holiday calendar covers every day of the year. The guides and app lists cover the subjects our own apps are about: sleep sounds, hot tub care, invoices, signatures, affirmations, reaction games and daily holidays.

How we check holiday dates

The calendar began as the data inside our app CelebriDay. Before publishing a holiday with a fixed date here, we checked the date against a public source. Where that source is on file, the day page links to it. Holidays we could not confirm were left off.

Holidays that move, such as Easter or Labor Day, are worked out from their rule and shown on their next date. The calendar is rebuilt every day. Many national days are informal and have no official body behind them, so sources sometimes disagree.

How we choose apps for our lists

Our lists include apps we make, so we have an interest in them. Each list states what we looked for and gives other apps their own strengths and prices.

How the site earns money

Our apps have paid versions, and we take on client work. Some product links on the hot tub pages are Amazon affiliate links, marked on the page. The privacy policy covers advertising and cookies.

Contact and corrections

Found a wrong date, a broken tool or an outdated price? Write to ramsey@graysmithlabs.com with the page address. For projects, use the contact form.
