How to use a daily affirmation

  1. Say it out loud. Speaking the words slowly, in the first person, keeps your attention on them better than reading in your head.
  2. Pair it with something you already do. Say it while the kettle boils, after you brush your teeth or before you open your messages.
  3. Bring it back once in the day. When the moment it speaks to arrives, a meeting or a hard conversation, repeat it once before you start.
  4. Keep the streak. One line a day for a week does more than a long list read once. Come back tomorrow for a new one.

Questions

What is today's affirmation?

Today's affirmation is shown at the top of this page and changes at midnight on your device. Every visitor sees the same one that day, picked from SayBright's 40 confidence affirmations.

How do I use a daily affirmation?

Read it out loud once in the morning, slowly, in the first person. Say it again when the moment it speaks to comes up during the day. Repeating one line every day for a week works better than reading many lines once.

Can I share the affirmation on Instagram Stories?

Yes. Tap Share card to make a 1080 by 1920 image of today's affirmation. On a phone it opens the share sheet, so you can post it to Stories or send it in a message; on a computer it downloads the image.

Do affirmations work?

Studies of self affirmation suggest that reflecting on what you value can ease stress and help people stay open to hard feedback. Affirmations are not a treatment for anxiety or depression; if those are weighing on you, talk to a professional.

Is there an affirmation app with widgets?

SayBright for iPhone gives five affirmations a day for free, the whole Confidence category, reminders and streaks. Premium adds every category, home and lock screen widgets and recordings in your own voice.
