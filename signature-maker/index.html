How to make an electronic signature

  1. Draw or type. On a phone, sign with your finger in the box above. On a computer, use a mouse, trackpad or stylus. If drawing feels awkward, switch to Type and pick the script font that looks most like you.
  2. Pick the ink. Black works everywhere. Blue makes it obvious on a printout which copy carries the original signature.
  3. Download the PNG. The file has a transparent background and is trimmed to the ink, so it drops onto a signature line without a white box around it.
  4. Place it on the document. In Word or Google Docs, insert it as an image and set it in front of the text. In a PDF, use your PDF app's image or signature tool and drag it onto the line.

Tips for a signature that looks right

  • Sign bigger than feels natural and fill most of the box. The PNG is trimmed and scaled down on the document, which hides small wobbles.
  • Sign in one or two quick strokes. Slow, careful signatures look shaky; your real one is fast.
  • Use Undo to take back the last stroke instead of starting over.
  • Keep a drawn signature for leases, contracts and offer letters, and a typed one for quick forms and permission slips.

Questions

Is this signature maker free?

Yes. There is no account, no watermark and no limit. The signature is drawn and saved in your browser, and nothing is sent to a server.

How do I get a transparent signature PNG?

Draw or type your signature and tap Download PNG. The file has a transparent background and is trimmed to the ink, so it sits cleanly on the signature line of any document.

Is a drawn or typed electronic signature legally valid?

In the United States the ESIGN Act and UETA treat an electronic signature as valid for most contracts when the signer means to sign, and the European Union has similar rules under eIDAS. Some documents, such as wills and certain court or notarized papers, still need a wet or certified signature. If a document is important, check what the other party accepts.

How do I add the signature to a PDF on iPhone?

Save the PNG to Photos, then open the PDF in Files, tap Markup, tap the plus button and add the image, or use a signing app such as SignSnap, which keeps your signature ready and places it on the line with the date.

Should my typed signature match my handwritten one?

It does not have to. What matters is that you chose to sign. Many people use a drawn signature for contracts and a typed one for quick forms.
