What is inside

The parts every paid app needs.

The same pieces run in Drift, SignSnap, CelebriDay, SayBright and BlitzTap today.

RevenueCat paywall

Plans read live from RevenueCat with annual first, free trial copy only when Apple reports the user eligible, a retry state instead of an endless spinner, restore, and the legal lines Guideline 3.1.2 asks for.

AdMob done fairly

App Tracking Transparency asked after onboarding, personalized ads only when tracking is allowed, capped interstitials that never interrupt the core experience, a banner that never shifts the layout, and a guard against test ad ids in production.

Analytics events

A fixed list of onboarding, paywall and purchase events sent to PostHog over fetch, switched on by one environment variable and off when it is missing.

Screenshot kit

App Store screenshots for iPhone 6.9 inch, 6.1 inch and iPad 13 inch made from the Expo web export in one command, with captions, and a check that fails any frame showing a price, a trial or a paywall (Guideline 2.3.7).

Build, submit and listings

EAS Build and Submit profiles, over the air updates, and the App Store listing kept in store.config.json and pushed with EAS Metadata.

Guard hooks and Claude subagents

Claude Code hooks that check commit authorship, store field limits and test ad ids on every edit, plus subagents for listing copy, UX review, monetization and a release audit you run before every build.

Who it is for

Indie builders who want to ship, not set up.

You know React Native or want to learn it, you plan to charge for your app with a subscription or ads, and you would rather spend your first week on the product than on paywalls, tracking prompts and App Review rejections.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Just ask.

What is the Graysmith Labs starter kit?

An Expo React Native template for subscription and ad supported iPhone apps, taken from the code and checks behind the Graysmith Labs App Store apps. It includes a RevenueCat paywall, AdMob with App Tracking Transparency, analytics, App Store screenshot automation, EAS build and submit, and release checks.

Is the kit available now?

Not yet. It is being prepared from the template the studio already uses, and the waitlist decides whether it ships. People on the waitlist hear first when it is ready.

How much will it cost?

There is no price yet. It will be on the page before anyone can buy, and joining the waitlist costs nothing and commits you to nothing.

Which stack does it use?

Expo managed React Native with TypeScript, EAS Build, Update and Submit, RevenueCat for subscriptions with an entitlement named premium, Google AdMob for ads and PostHog for analytics.

Why would I trust it with App Review?

Every check in it comes from a real App Store review the studio went through, such as a rejection for a paywall frame in screenshots (Guideline 2.3.7) and one for a reviewer who could not find the subscriptions (Guideline 2.1). The release check looks for those before you submit.
