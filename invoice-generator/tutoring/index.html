How to bill tutoring

  1. Bill monthly with the session dates. Parents like one invoice a month. Listing each date in the line lets them check it against their calendar.
  2. Charge in hours, including half hours. A quantity of 1.5 at your hourly rate is clearer than a new rate for every length of session.
  3. Name the student and the subject. Families with more than one child, or a parent paying for a sibling, need to see who each line is for.
  4. State your cancellation rule. Late cancels and no shows are the most common tutoring dispute. Put the rule in the notes and bill it as a line when it applies.

Questions

What should a tutoring invoice include?

Your name and contact details, the parent or client, the student's name, the subject, each session date with its length, your hourly rate, any materials, the total, the due date and how to pay.

Should tutors bill per session or per month?

Per month for regular students, listing every session, so parents pay once. Per session suits one off help and test prep before an exam.

Can I charge for late cancellations as a tutor?

Yes, if you told the family the rule before the first session. Common rules charge the full session when a cancel comes less than 24 hours ahead.
