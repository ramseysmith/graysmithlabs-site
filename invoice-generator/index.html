How to make an invoice

  1. Add your business. Your name and how to reach you go at the top, so the client knows who to pay.
  2. Add the client. Their name and address or email go under Bill to.
  3. Number and date it. Use a new number for every invoice, like INV-0001, INV-0002, and set a due date, often 14 days after the job.
  4. List what you did. One line per item with a quantity and rate, such as 2 hours at 40. The total adds itself up, with tax if you charge it.
  5. Say how to pay. Venmo, Zelle, a check or a pay link. Invoices with clear payment details get paid sooner.
  6. Download and send. Save the PDF and send it by email or text the same day as the job.

Questions

Is this invoice generator really free?

Yes. There is no account, no watermark and no limit on how many invoices you make. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

How do I save the invoice as a PDF?

Tap Download PDF. Your browser opens its print window; choose Save as PDF (on iPhone, pinch out on the preview and tap Share, then Save to Files). Only the invoice is printed, not this page.

What should an invoice include?

Your business name and contact details, the client's name, an invoice number, the date issued and the due date, each item with quantity and rate, the total, and how to pay. This generator fills in every one of those.

What is the difference between an invoice and a receipt?

An invoice asks to be paid; a receipt confirms payment arrived. Send the invoice when the work is done, and a receipt once the client pays.

How do I get paid faster?

Send the invoice the same day as the job, set a clear due date, include a pay link or payment instructions, and send a friendly reminder the day after it is due. The Tallyhand iPhone app does those steps for you.
