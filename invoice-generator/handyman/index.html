How to bill handyman work

  1. Separate labor from materials. Clients accept a materials line with receipts much more easily than one number that hides them. Add your markup to the materials price if you charge one.
  2. Use a minimum or a trip charge. Small jobs still cost you the drive. A one hour minimum or a flat trip charge on its own line keeps short calls worth taking.
  3. List the tasks, not just the hours. Two doors hung and one patch is clearer than 3 hours, and it reminds the client of everything you did.
  4. Send an estimate first on bigger jobs. For anything over a few hours, an estimate the client accepts becomes the invoice when you finish, with the same lines.

Questions

What should a handyman invoice include?

Your business name and contact details, the client and job address, the date, each task or the labor hours with your rate, materials with their cost, any trip charge, the total, the due date and how to pay.

How do handymen charge for small jobs?

With a minimum, usually one or two hours of labor, or a flat trip charge plus time. Put it on the invoice as its own line so the client sees why a ten minute fix has a minimum.

Should I mark up materials on a handyman invoice?

Many handymen add 10 to 20 percent to cover buying, driving and returns. Either build it into each materials price or add it as its own line; just be consistent.
