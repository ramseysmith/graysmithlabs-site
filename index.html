FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Just ask.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope, so every project gets a written estimate after a free scoping call. Defined builds get a fixed price. Contract engineering is billed at a weekly or monthly rate.

How long does it take?

The timeline is set in the written plan before work starts. Because we share working builds regularly, you see real progress from the first week, not just status reports.

Who owns the code?

You do. Once the work is paid for, the code, designs, and accounts belong to your company, and everything is documented so any engineer can pick it up.

Can you work inside our existing codebase and team?

Yes. Contract engineering is built for exactly that: we join your repos, tools, and review process and work like any other member of the team.

What happens after launch?

Software needs care after it ships. A monthly care plan covers operating system and store updates, fixes, monitoring, and ongoing improvements guided by real usage.

How do you use AI?

AI accelerates and enhances the work. Every change is still reviewed and owned by the engineer doing it, so you get the speed without giving up quality.
