What holiday is it on June 14?

June 14 is Flag Day and National Strawberry Shortcake Day. It falls on a Sunday in 2026 and a Monday in 2027.

Written and illustrated by Graysmith Labs, the makers of CelebriDay.

Culture

Flag Day

A day commemorating the adoption of the flag of the United States on June 14, 1777.

On June 14, 1777, the Continental Congress resolved that the flag be thirteen stripes, alternate red and white, with thirteen stars in a blue field. It did not say how the stars should be arranged, so early flags vary.

Wisconsin teacher Bernard Cigrand held a flag birthday in his classroom in 1885 and spent years campaigning for the day. President Wilson proclaimed it in 1916 and Congress made it National Flag Day in 1949. The design with fifty stars began as a high school project by Robert Heft of Ohio.

Ways to celebrate

  • Learn the triangle fold.
  • Retire a worn flag through a local veterans post.
  • Count the stars in an old photograph to date it.
