What holiday is it on December 17?

December 17 is National Maple Syrup Day and Wright Brothers Day. It falls on a Thursday in 2026 and a Friday in 2027.

Food

National Maple Syrup Day

A sticky sweet celebration of one of nature's most delicious gifts, the golden syrup tapped from maple trees.

Nobody is sure who started National Maple Syrup Day, but the syrup itself has deep roots. Indigenous peoples of northeastern North America were making maple sugar long before Europeans arrived, and they taught early settlers how to tap the trees.

Sap runs best in late winter and early spring, when nights freeze and days thaw. Those swings in temperature build pressure inside the tree and push sap out of the tap. Quebec produces most of the world's maple syrup, and syrup is graded by color and flavor, from golden and delicate to very dark and strong.

Ways to celebrate

  • Make pancakes with real maple syrup.
  • Glaze carrots or salmon with maple.
  • Compare a light and a dark syrup grade.

Tech

Wright Brothers Day

A day commemorating the first successful powered airplane flight by Orville and Wilbur Wright on December 17, 1903.

On December 17, 1903, at Kill Devil Hills near Kitty Hawk, North Carolina, Orville and Wilbur Wright made the first sustained, controlled, powered airplane flights. Congress first named Wright Brothers Day in 1959, and presidents now proclaim it every year.

The brothers ran a bicycle shop in Dayton, Ohio, and taught themselves aeronautics through careful experiments, including a homemade wind tunnel. They flew four times that day. The longest flight, piloted by Wilbur, covered 852 feet in 59 seconds. Their key insight was control: steering by twisting the wings, a method called wing warping.

Ways to celebrate

  • Fold paper airplanes and test which flies farthest.
  • Visit a local air or space museum.
  • Read about the Wright brothers' wind tunnel tests.
