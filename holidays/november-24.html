What holiday is it on November 24?

November 24 is National Sardines Day and Celebrate Your Unique Talent Day. It falls on a Tuesday in 2026 and a Wednesday in 2027.

Written and illustrated by Graysmith Labs, the makers of CelebriDay.

Food

National Sardines Day

A fishy celebration of the small but mighty omega 3 packed sardine.

Nobody is sure who started National Sardines Day. The word sardine is not one single species. It is a name given to several small, oily fish in the herring family that swim in large schools and are often packed tightly in tins, which is where the phrase packed like sardines comes from.

Canning sardines was once a major industry along the coast of Maine, with dozens of canneries. The last one in the United States, in Prospect Harbor, Maine, closed in 2010. Sardines are still popular across Portugal and Spain, where they are often grilled whole.

Ways to celebrate

  • Mash sardines on toast with lemon and pepper.
  • Grill fresh sardines if your market has them.
  • Toss tinned sardines into a simple pasta.

Celebrate Your Unique Talent Day

A fun day to show off the special skills and talents that make you one of a kind.

Nobody is sure who created Celebrate Your Unique Talent Day, but its message is easy to like. Everyone has something they do well, whether it is whistling, baking, fixing bikes, drawing or making people laugh. The day invites you to share that skill instead of keeping it to yourself.

Talents are rarely fixed at birth. Most skills grow through practice, and many people discover a talent only after trying something new. A talent does not have to be useful or impressive to be worth celebrating. Sometimes the odd little skill is the one people remember most about you.

Ways to celebrate

  • Show a friend a skill they have never seen.
  • Teach someone one thing you are good at.
  • Try a beginner class in something new.
