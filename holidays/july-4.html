What holiday is it on July 4?

July 4 is Independence Day. It falls on a Saturday in 2026 and a Sunday in 2027.

Written and illustrated by Graysmith Labs, the makers of CelebriDay.

Culture

Independence Day

Independence Day commemorates the adoption of the Declaration of Independence on July 4, 1776, marking the birth of the United States of America.

The Continental Congress voted for independence on July 2, 1776, and adopted the wording of the Declaration two days later. John Adams expected Americans to celebrate the second. Most delegates did not sign the parchment copy until August 2.

Philadelphia set off fireworks for the first anniversary in 1777. Adams and Thomas Jefferson both died on July 4, 1826, the fiftieth anniversary, a coincidence that astonished the country.

Ways to celebrate

  • Read the Declaration's second paragraph out loud before dinner.
  • Watch fireworks from farther back than usual; the view is wider and the sound gentler.
  • Keep pets indoors with a fan or steady noise running.
