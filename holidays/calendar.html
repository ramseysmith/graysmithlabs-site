How to add it

iPhone or iPad

Open this page in Safari and tap Add to Apple Calendar, then Subscribe. Or go to Settings, Apps, Calendar, Calendar Accounts, Add Account, Other, Add Subscribed Calendar, and paste the feed address.

Mac

In Calendar, choose File, New Calendar Subscription, paste the feed address and click Subscribe. Set Auto refresh to Every day.

Google Calendar

Click Add to Google Calendar, then Add. Or in Google Calendar on a computer, open Other calendars, the plus sign, From URL, and paste the feed address. It then shows on your phone too.

Outlook

In Outlook on the web, open Add calendar, Subscribe from web, paste the feed address and name it National Days.

Questions

Is the national day calendar free?

Yes. The feed is free, has no account and no ads inside the events. Subscribe once and it keeps itself up to date.

How do I add national days to my iPhone calendar?

Tap the Add to Apple Calendar button on this page on your iPhone, then tap Subscribe. The days show up in the Calendar app under National Days, and you can turn them off any time in Calendars.

How do I add national days to Google Calendar?

On a computer, click Add to Google Calendar on this page, then Add. Google refreshes subscribed calendars on its own schedule, usually within a day.

Does the calendar update by itself?

Yes. It is rebuilt every morning and always covers the past month and the year ahead, so moving days like Thanksgiving and Mother's Day land on the right date each year without you doing anything.

Which days are in the feed?

The same days as our holiday pages: federal holidays, awareness days, food days and fun days, each one published only after its date was checked at a source we can name.

Can I put this calendar on my website or in a newsletter?

Yes. Link to this page or to the feed address. Teachers, social media managers and bloggers use it to plan posts and lessons.
