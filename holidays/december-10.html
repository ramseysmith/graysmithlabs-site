What holiday is it on December 10?

December 10 is National Lager Day and Human Rights Day. It falls on a Thursday in 2026 and a Friday in 2027.

Food

National Lager Day

A day to raise a glass to lager, the world's most popular style of beer, known for its crisp and refreshing taste.

Nobody is sure who started National Lager Day. Lager is defined by its yeast and its cold. Lager yeast ferments at cooler temperatures and settles at the bottom of the tank, and the beer is then stored cold for weeks, which gives it a clean, crisp taste.

One of the most important moments for lager came in 1842 in the Czech city of Plzeň, where brewer Josef Groll made the first golden pilsner. Its clear golden color looked striking in a glass, and pale lagers went on to become the most widely brewed beers in the world.

Ways to celebrate

  • Taste a pilsner next to a dark lager.
  • Cook a dish with beer, like beer bread.
  • Choose a nonalcoholic lager if you prefer.

Human Rights Day

A United Nations observance marking the adoption of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights in 1948.

Human Rights Day marks December 10, 1948, when the United Nations General Assembly adopted the Universal Declaration of Human Rights in Paris. The drafting committee was chaired by Eleanor Roosevelt, and members came from many countries and traditions.

The Declaration has 30 articles setting out rights that belong to every person, including freedom from torture, the right to a fair trial, freedom of speech and the right to education. It is not itself a treaty, but it inspired many binding laws and treaties and has shaped constitutions around the world.

Ways to celebrate

  • Read the 30 articles of the Declaration.
  • Support a human rights organization you trust.
  • Talk with family about which right matters most.
