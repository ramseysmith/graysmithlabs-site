What holiday is it on January 31?

January 31 is Backwards Day and Inspire Your Heart with Art Day. It falls on a Saturday in 2026 and a Sunday in 2027.

Written and illustrated by Graysmith Labs, the makers of CelebriDay.

Fun

Backwards Day

A topsy turvy holiday where everything is done in reverse, from wearing clothes backwards to reading words right to left.

Same date every year. Date checked against timeanddate.com. How we check.

Arts

Inspire Your Heart with Art Day

A day encouraging people to connect with art in any form and let it move, uplift, and inspire their hearts.

Same date every year. Date checked against brandywine.org. How we check.
