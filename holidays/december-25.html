What holiday is it on December 25?

December 25 is Christmas Day and National Pumpkin Pie Day. It falls on a Friday in 2026 and a Saturday in 2027.

Culture

Christmas Day

The most widely celebrated holiday in the world, commemorating the birth of Jesus Christ and observed with gifts, feasting, and family.

Christians celebrate Christmas as the birth of Jesus. The Bible does not give a date, and the earliest record of December 25 as the celebration comes from Rome in the year 336. Over the centuries the day picked up customs from many places, including evergreen trees from Germany and Santa Claus from Dutch and American traditions.

In the United States, Christmas became a federal holiday in 1870. Today it is celebrated by people of many faiths and none, with church services, family meals, gift giving and quiet time together. Many people also volunteer or reach out to those spending the day alone.

Ways to celebrate

  • Call someone who is spending today alone.
  • Share a meal with family or friends.
  • Take a walk to see the lights.

Food

National Pumpkin Pie Day

A day celebrating the beloved spiced pumpkin custard pie that graces holiday tables across America.

Nobody is sure who started National Pumpkin Pie Day, but pumpkin pie has a long American story. One of the first American cookbooks, Amelia Simmons's American Cookery from 1796, included a pompkin pudding baked in a crust, which is close to the pie we know.

Pumpkin pie is really a custard. Eggs, milk or cream and pumpkin set together in the oven, flavored with cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg and cloves. A common tip is to pull the pie out while the center still jiggles slightly, since it keeps cooking as it cools and cracks if it bakes too long.

Ways to celebrate

  • Bake a pumpkin pie from scratch.
  • Make your own pumpkin pie spice blend.
  • Top a slice with fresh whipped cream.
