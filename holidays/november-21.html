What holiday is it on November 21?

November 21 is National Stuffing Day and World Hello Day. It falls on a Saturday in 2026 and a Sunday in 2027.

Food

National Stuffing Day

A savory celebration of the classic holiday side dish made with seasoned bread.

Nobody is sure who started National Stuffing Day, but its timing is no mystery: it lands right before Thanksgiving, when stuffing claims its spot on millions of plates. Whether you call it stuffing or dressing often depends on where you grew up, and some cooks use the words to mean baked inside the bird or baked in a pan.

Regional versions say a lot about a place. The South leans on cornbread, New England cooks may add oysters, and many families add sausage, chestnuts or apples. Day old bread works best because dry bread soaks up broth without turning to mush.

Ways to celebrate

  • Bake a pan of stuffing with a new mix in.
  • Call a relative and ask for their stuffing recipe.
  • Turn leftover bread into croutons or stuffing.

Fun

World Hello Day

A fun day encouraging people to greet 10 strangers to promote peace and communication.

World Hello Day was started in 1973 by two brothers, Brian and Michael McCormack, as a response to the conflict in the Middle East that year. Their idea was simple: if leaders and ordinary people talk to each other, conflicts are easier to resolve. They asked people to greet ten others on the day as a small act for peace.

The idea spread through letters, and over the years many well known figures, including Nobel Peace Prize winners, sent messages of support. The day is a reminder that a single word can open a door between strangers.

Ways to celebrate

  • Say hello to ten people today.
  • Learn how to say hello in a new language.
  • Message a friend you have not talked to lately.
