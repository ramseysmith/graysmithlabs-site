What holiday is it on November 11?

November 11 is Veterans Day and National Sundae Day. It falls on a Wednesday in 2026 and a Thursday in 2027.

Written and illustrated by Graysmith Labs, the makers of CelebriDay.

Culture

Veterans Day

A federal holiday honoring military veterans who have served in the US armed forces.

The fighting of the First World War stopped at the eleventh hour of the eleventh day of the eleventh month in 1918. The date was kept as Armistice Day until 1954, when Congress renamed it Veterans Day to honor everyone who has served in the US armed forces.

It is not the same as Memorial Day, which remembers those who died in service. In 1971 the holiday was moved to a Monday in October; the change was so unpopular that it returned to November 11 in 1978. There is no apostrophe: the day belongs to all veterans.

Ways to celebrate

  • Call a veteran you know and ask about the people they served with.
  • Write down a family member's service story while you can still ask.
  • Pause for a minute at 11 in the morning.
