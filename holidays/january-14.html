What holiday is it on January 14?

January 14 is Organize Your Home Day and Hot Pastrami Sandwich Day. It falls on a Wednesday in 2026 and a Thursday in 2027.

Fun

Organize Your Home Day

A day dedicated to decluttering and organizing your living space for a fresh and functional start to the year.

Nobody is sure who started Organize Your Home Day, but mid January is a natural time for it. The holiday decorations are packed away, new gifts need a place to live and many people want their home to match the fresh start they hoped for on New Year's Day.

Professional organizers often suggest a simple rule: give everything a home, and put things back there right after you use them. Another popular method is to sort one category at a time, such as all your books or all your shoes, rather than one room at a time, so you can see how much you really own. Starting with a single drawer is often easier than tackling a whole closet.

Ways to celebrate

  • Clear out one drawer completely.
  • Donate clothes you have not worn in a year.
  • Label shelves so everything has a home.

Hot Pastrami Sandwich Day

A delicious holiday honoring the classic hot pastrami sandwich, a cornerstone of deli culture.

Nobody is sure who started Hot Pastrami Sandwich Day, but the sandwich belongs to the Jewish delis of New York. Many accounts credit Sussman Volk, a Lithuanian immigrant, with selling one of the city's first pastrami sandwiches in the late 1880s, though the story is hard to confirm.

Pastrami starts as beef, usually brisket or navel, which is cured in brine, rubbed with black pepper and coriander, smoked and then steamed until tender. The name comes from the Romanian pastrama, a pressed and cured meat with roots in Turkish cooking. The classic is pastrami on rye with mustard, sliced by hand and piled high, served hot.

Ways to celebrate

  • Build a hot pastrami on rye with mustard.
  • Visit a local deli you have never tried.
  • Learn how corned beef and pastrami differ.
