What holiday is it on December 11?

December 11 is National Noodle Ring Day and International Mountain Day. It falls on a Friday in 2026 and a Saturday in 2027.

Written and illustrated by Graysmith Labs, the makers of CelebriDay.

Food

National Noodle Ring Day

A quirky day celebrating the classic mid century baked noodle ring dish that was a staple of American cooking.

Nobody is sure who started National Noodle Ring Day, which honors a dish that has mostly faded from dinner tables. A noodle ring is cooked egg noodles mixed with eggs, milk and cheese, then baked in a ring mold until set. Once unmolded, the center was often filled with creamed chicken, peas or another savory sauce.

Ring molds were popular for showy home cooking in the middle of the twentieth century, when gelatin salads and molded dishes signaled a special meal. Making a noodle ring today is a fun way to taste the history of American home cooking.

Ways to celebrate

  • Bake a noodle ring in a bundt pan.
  • Fill the center with creamed chicken or mushrooms.
  • Look through an old family cookbook.

Nature

International Mountain Day

A United Nations day raising awareness of the importance of mountains for life, water, and biodiversity.

The United Nations named 2002 the International Year of Mountains, and the General Assembly then designated December 11 as International Mountain Day, first observed in 2003. It draws attention to the people who live in the mountains and to the land itself.

Mountains matter far beyond their slopes. Snow and glaciers on high peaks feed rivers that supply water to a huge share of humanity, and mountain regions hold a rich variety of plants and animals. They are also sensitive to climate change, with glaciers shrinking in many ranges around the world.

Ways to celebrate

  • Take a hike, even a short one.
  • Watch a documentary about mountain communities.
  • Learn where your tap water comes from.
