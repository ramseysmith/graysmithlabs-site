What holiday is it on December 19?

December 19 is National Hard Candy Day and Look for an Evergreen Day. It falls on a Saturday in 2026 and a Sunday in 2027.

Written and illustrated by Graysmith Labs, the makers of CelebriDay.

Food

National Hard Candy Day

A sweet and colorful day celebrating hard candies from classic peppermints to jolly ranchers and everything in between.

Nobody is sure who started National Hard Candy Day, but it fits the season of candy canes, ribbon candy and peppermints. Hard candy is mostly sugar boiled until almost all the water is gone, then cooled into a glassy solid.

Candy makers call that point the hard crack stage, around 300 degrees Fahrenheit. At that temperature, sugar syrup dropped into cold water snaps instead of bending. Because there is so little water left, hard candy keeps for a long time. Humidity is its enemy, since moisture from the air makes the surface sticky.

Ways to celebrate

  • Make lollipops or hard candy at home.
  • Build a candy cane jar for guests.
  • Try a hard candy from another country.

Nature

Look for an Evergreen Day

A day encouraging people to go outside and appreciate the enduring beauty of evergreen trees and plants in winter.

Nobody is sure who started Look for an Evergreen Day, but it is perfect for a winter walk. When most trees stand bare, pines, spruces, firs, hollies and junipers stay green and give birds shelter and food.

Evergreens keep their leaves through winter thanks to some clever features. Narrow needles and waxy coatings help them hold on to water when the ground is frozen, and many contain natural compounds that keep their cells from freezing solid. They do shed needles, just a few at a time instead of all at once.

Ways to celebrate

  • Take a walk and name three evergreens.
  • Bring home fallen branches for a wreath.
  • Plant an evergreen in the spring.

Same date every year. Date checked against nationaltoday.com. How we check.
