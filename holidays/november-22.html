What holiday is it on November 22?

November 22 is National Cranberry Relish Day and Go for a Ride Day. It falls on a Sunday in 2026 and a Monday in 2027.

Written and illustrated by Graysmith Labs, the makers of CelebriDay.

Food

National Cranberry Relish Day

A tart and sweet celebration of the classic cranberry condiment perfect for the holidays.

Nobody knows for sure who created National Cranberry Relish Day, but it sits right where it belongs, just before Thanksgiving. Unlike cooked cranberry sauce, many relish recipes use raw cranberries chopped with orange, sugar and sometimes nuts, which keeps the flavor bright and sharp.

Cranberries grow on low vines in sandy bogs. At harvest many farmers flood the bogs, shake the berries loose and let them float, since pockets of air inside each berry keep it buoyant. Those air pockets also make fresh cranberries bounce, which growers have long used to sort good berries from soft ones.

Ways to celebrate

  • Make a raw cranberry orange relish.
  • Spoon cranberry relish onto a turkey sandwich.
  • Drop a fresh cranberry and watch it bounce.

Fun

Go for a Ride Day

A fun day encouraging everyone to hop in the car or on a bike and enjoy a scenic ride.

Nobody is sure who started Go for a Ride Day, and that seems fitting for a day with no destination. The idea is simply to take a ride for the joy of it, whether in a car, on a bike, on a train or on a horse, and notice the world going by.

A ride with no errand attached has a long history. In the early days of the automobile, a Sunday drive was a popular outing in itself. Today a slow ride on a back road or a bike path can be a cheap way to clear your head.

Ways to celebrate

  • Take a scenic drive with a good playlist.
  • Ride a bike around a park or trail.
  • Hop on a local bus or train somewhere new.

Same date every year. Date checked against nbcpalmsprings.com. How we check.
