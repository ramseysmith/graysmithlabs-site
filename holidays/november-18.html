What holiday is it on November 18?

November 18 is National Vichyssoise Day and National Apple Cider Day. It falls on a Wednesday in 2026 and a Thursday in 2027.

Written and illustrated by Graysmith Labs, the makers of CelebriDay.

Food

National Vichyssoise Day

A day to enjoy the classic cold French potato and leek soup.

Vichyssoise is a cold purée of leeks, potatoes and cream, and despite its French name it is best known from New York. Louis Diat, chef at the Ritz Carlton, said he based it on the hot soup of his childhood, which he and his brother cooled with milk in summer.

He named it after Vichy, a spa town near his home. The final s sound is pronounced: vee shee swahz.

Ways to celebrate

  • Make it the night before, since cold soup needs time for flavor.
  • Season again after chilling; cold dulls salt.
  • Serve it hot and call it leek and potato.

Same date every year. Date checked against nationaldaycalendar.com. How we check.

Food

National Apple Cider Day

A cozy celebration of the warm and spiced autumn beverage made from apples.

In the United States cider usually means fresh, unfiltered apple juice; in most of the world it means a fermented drink. Colonists drank the hard kind daily, and many early American orchards were planted for pressing rather than eating.

The brown color is oxygen at work on the pressed fruit, the same thing that happens to a sliced apple on the counter.

Ways to celebrate

  • Warm a pot with cinnamon sticks and orange peel.
  • Reduce a quart to a syrup for pancakes.
  • Visit a press while they are still running.

Same date every year. Date checked against visitnewportbeach.com. How we check.
