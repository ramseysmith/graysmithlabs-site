What holiday is it on March 12?

March 12 is National Plant a Flower Day and Girl Scout Day. It falls on a Thursday in 2026 and a Friday in 2027.

Written and illustrated by Graysmith Labs, the makers of CelebriDay.

Nature

National Plant a Flower Day

A day encouraging people to get their hands in the soil and plant flowers to welcome the coming spring season.

Same date every year. Date checked against vegogarden.com. How we check.

Culture

Girl Scout Day

Commemorating the founding of the Girl Scouts of the USA by Juliette Gordon Low on March 12, 1912.

Same date every year. Date checked against wsls.com. How we check.
