What holiday is it on June 20?

June 20 is Father's Day, World Refugee Day, National Vanilla Milkshake Day and National Ice Cream Soda Day. It falls on a Saturday in 2026 and a Sunday in 2027.

Written and illustrated by Graysmith Labs, the makers of CelebriDay.

Culture

Father's Day

A day to celebrate dads and father figures for their guidance, humor, and support.

Sonora Smart Dodd of Spokane, Washington proposed the day after hearing a Mother's Day sermon. Her father had raised six children alone. Spokane held the first one on June 19, 1910.

It took decades to catch on. President Nixon signed it into law in 1972, on the third Sunday in June.

Ways to celebrate

  • Ask him to teach you the one thing he is best at.
  • Look through old photos and write the dates on the back.
  • Let him pick the music.

The date moves each year. In 2027 it falls on Sunday, June 20. After that: June 18 in 2028, June 17 in 2029, June 16 in 2030 and June 15 in 2031.
