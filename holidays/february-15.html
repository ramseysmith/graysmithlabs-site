What holiday is it on February 15?

February 15 is Presidents' Day, National Gumdrop Day and Singles Awareness Day. It falls on a Sunday in 2026 and a Monday in 2027.

Written and illustrated by Graysmith Labs, the makers of CelebriDay.

Culture

Presidents' Day

A federal holiday honoring George Washington and all the presidents who have led the United States.

Presidents Day began as Washington's Birthday, celebrated on February 22 and made a holiday for all federal offices in 1885. In 1971 the Uniform Monday Holiday Act moved it to the third Monday in February, which gives workers a long weekend but means it never falls on Washington's actual birthday.

The official federal name is still Washington's Birthday, but many states and stores call it Presidents Day, and many use it to honor Abraham Lincoln too, whose birthday is February 12.

Ways to celebrate

  • Read a short biography of a president you know little about.
  • Visit a presidential library, home or memorial.
  • Quiz your family with presidential trivia.

The date moves each year. In 2027 it falls on Monday, February 15. After that: February 21 in 2028, February 19 in 2029, February 18 in 2030 and February 17 in 2031.
