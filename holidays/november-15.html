What holiday is it on November 15?

November 15 is National Bundt Cake Day and America Recycles Day. It falls on a Sunday in 2026 and a Monday in 2027.

Written and illustrated by Graysmith Labs, the makers of CelebriDay.

Food

National Bundt Cake Day

A celebration of the iconic ring shaped cake with its beautiful fluted design.

H. David Dalquist of Nordic Ware made the first aluminum Bundt pan in Minnesota around 1950, after a group of women asked him for a lighter version of a European ceramic mold. It sold slowly for years.

Then, in 1966, a Tunnel of Fudge cake baked in one placed second in the Pillsbury Bake Off, and the pan became a kitchen staple. The hole is practical: it lets heat reach the center so a dense cake bakes evenly.

Ways to celebrate

  • Butter every groove, then dust with sugar instead of flour.
  • Wait ten minutes before turning the cake out.
  • Mail a slice of memory: send someone the recipe.

America Recycles Day

A national day promoting recycling and encouraging Americans to pledge to recycle more.

America Recycles Day began in 1997 to get people sorting more of what they throw away, and the White House has recognized it by proclamation. Aluminum is the great success of the bin, since it can be melted and remade again and again.

The most common mistake is hopeful recycling: greasy boxes, plastic bags and hoses that jam the sorting machines. An item that does not belong can spoil a whole load of things that do.

Ways to celebrate

  • Look up what your own town accepts; the rules differ street to street.
  • Take plastic bags back to a grocery store bin.
  • Rinse jars; a quick swirl is enough.
