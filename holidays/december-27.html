What holiday is it on December 27?

December 27 is National Fruitcake Day and Make a Cut Out Snowflake Day. It falls on a Sunday in 2026 and a Monday in 2027.

Written and illustrated by Graysmith Labs, the makers of CelebriDay.

Food

National Fruitcake Day

A day celebrating the dense, fruit filled holiday cake that has inspired both devoted fans and notorious jokes for centuries.

Nobody is sure who started National Fruitcake Day, but cakes packed with dried fruit, nuts and spices have been baked in Europe for centuries, partly because they kept so well through the winter.

Fruitcake keeps because sugar draws water out of the fruit and slows spoilage, and many bakers brush the cake with brandy or rum over several weeks. In Britain a rich fruitcake is the classic Christmas cake and wedding cake, often covered with marzipan and icing. The jokes are mostly an American habit.

Ways to celebrate

  • Bake a small fruitcake with dried fruits you like.
  • Try a slice with sharp cheese.
  • Give fruitcake one fair chance with friends.

Arts

Make a Cut Out Snowflake Day

A charming day encouraging people of all ages to create beautiful paper snowflakes using scissors and folded paper.

Nobody is sure who started Make a Cut Out Snowflake Day, but it is a perfect project for the quiet days after Christmas. Folding a paper square into a triangle and cutting shapes along the edges creates a design that repeats as you unfold it.

Real snowflakes have six sides because water molecules lock together in a six sided pattern as they freeze. Wilson Bentley, a farmer in Vermont, was the first person to photograph a single snowflake, in 1885. He took thousands of pictures over his life, which helped popularize the idea that no two look alike.

Ways to celebrate

  • Fold and cut six sided paper snowflakes.
  • Hang snowflakes on a window.
  • Look at Wilson Bentley's snowflake photos.

Same date every year. Date checked against celebratepicturebooks.com. How we check.
