What holiday is it on October 4?

October 4 is World Architecture Day, World Habitat Day, National Cinnamon Roll Day and World Animal Day. It falls on a Sunday in 2026 and a Monday in 2027.

Written and illustrated by Graysmith Labs, the makers of CelebriDay.

Arts

World Architecture Day

A global celebration of architecture and the built environment that shapes our world.

The date moves each year. In 2027 it falls on Monday, October 4. After that: October 2 in 2028, October 1 in 2029, October 7 in 2030 and October 6 in 2031.

Nature

World Habitat Day

A United Nations designated day to reflect on the state of human settlements and the right to adequate shelter.

The date moves each year. In 2027 it falls on Monday, October 4. After that: October 2 in 2028, October 1 in 2029, October 7 in 2030 and October 6 in 2031.

Food

National Cinnamon Roll Day

A sweet celebration of the beloved cinnamon roll, a warm and gooey pastry enjoyed worldwide.

Sweden has had a day for its cinnamon bun, the kanelbulle, every October 4 since 1999, when the Home Baking Council created it to mark its fortieth year. The American roll is a softer, sweeter cousin, usually under a layer of icing.

Swedish dough carries cardamom as well as cinnamon, and the buns are knotted or coiled and topped with pearl sugar.

Ways to celebrate

  • Add a teaspoon of ground cardamom to your dough.
  • Cut the rolls with plain dental floss for clean edges.
  • Invite a friend for fika, a coffee break with something sweet.

World Animal Day

An international day of action for animal rights and welfare, raising awareness for animals worldwide.

World Animal Day was first held in Berlin in 1925, organized by the writer Heinrich Zimmermann. It later settled on October 4, the feast day of Saint Francis of Assisi, who is remembered for his care for animals.

Churches in many countries hold a blessing of the animals around this date, with dogs, cats and the occasional goat or horse in line.

Ways to celebrate

  • Ask your local shelter what it is short of; it is often towels.
  • Put a shallow dish of fresh water out for birds.
  • Check that your pet's microchip has your current phone number.
