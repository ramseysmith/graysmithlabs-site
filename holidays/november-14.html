What holiday is it on November 14?

November 14 is National Pickle Day and World Diabetes Day. It falls on a Saturday in 2026 and a Sunday in 2027.

Written and illustrated by Graysmith Labs, the makers of CelebriDay.

Food

National Pickle Day

A briny celebration of pickles in all their delicious forms.

Pickling is one of the oldest ways to keep food, with roots thousands of years back in Mesopotamia. Salt water or vinegar makes the jar too sour for most spoilage, and the cucumber keeps its crunch for months.

Two methods share one name. Fermented pickles sour slowly in brine as friendly bacteria go to work; quick pickles get their bite from vinegar and are ready the next day.

Ways to celebrate

  • Slice cucumbers into hot vinegar, salt and dill tonight and eat them tomorrow.
  • Save the brine for a marinade.
  • Taste a half sour next to a full sour.

Health

World Diabetes Day

A global awareness day for diabetes education, prevention, and management.

November 14 is the birthday of Frederick Banting, who with Charles Best first isolated insulin in Toronto in 1921. The International Diabetes Federation and the World Health Organization created the day in 1991, and the United Nations adopted it in 2006.

Its symbol is a plain blue circle. Before insulin, type 1 diabetes was almost always fatal within a few years; the first patient, Leonard Thompson, was treated in January 1922.

Ways to celebrate

  • Learn the early signs: thirst, frequent urination, tiredness and weight loss.
  • Ask at your next checkup whether a blood sugar test makes sense for you.
  • Wear something blue.
