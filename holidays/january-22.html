What holiday is it on January 22?

January 22 is National Blonde Brownie Day and Answer Your Cat's Questions Day. It falls on a Thursday in 2026 and a Friday in 2027.

Written and illustrated by Graysmith Labs, the makers of CelebriDay.

Food

National Blonde Brownie Day

A day to enjoy the blondie, a delicious butterscotch flavored cousin of the traditional chocolate brownie.

Same date every year. Date checked against nationaldaycalendar.com. How we check.

Answer Your Cat's Questions Day

A whimsical holiday inviting cat owners to imagine what their feline friends might be curious about and answer their unspoken questions.

Same date every year. Date checked against timeanddate.com. How we check.
