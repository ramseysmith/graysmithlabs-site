Fun

Black Friday

The day after Thanksgiving and the traditional kickoff to the holiday shopping season.

Black Friday is the day after Thanksgiving, and for decades it has marked the start of the holiday shopping season in the United States. Philadelphia police used the name in the 1950s and 1960s for the crowds and traffic that jammed the city that day.

A later explanation says it is the day stores move from red ink to black and turn a profit, a story retailers liked better. Today the deals start earlier and run online all weekend.

Ways to celebrate

  • Make a list and a budget before you shop.
  • Shop at a local small business instead of a big chain.
  • Skip the stores and take a walk or watch a movie.

The date moves each year. In 2026 it falls on Friday, November 27. After that: November 26 in 2027, November 24 in 2028, November 23 in 2029 and November 29 in 2030.

Food

National Bavarian Cream Pie Day

A day celebrating the rich and creamy custard filled dessert of German origin.

Nobody is sure who started National Bavarian Cream Pie Day. Bavarian cream is a rich custard set with gelatin and lightened with whipped cream, and the classic dessert was usually shaped in a mold. Over time cooks began spooning it into a pie crust instead.

The cream gets its silky texture from careful cooking. The custard base of egg yolks, milk and sugar is heated gently so the eggs thicken without scrambling, then cooled before the whipped cream is folded in. A Bavarian cream pie is often topped with fruit or chocolate, and it is served cold.

Ways to celebrate

  • Make a Bavarian cream pie with fresh berries.
  • Practice folding whipped cream into custard.
  • Share a slice with someone after dinner.

Food

National Craft Jerky Day

A savory celebration of artisan and small batch dried meat snacks.

Nobody is sure who first named National Craft Jerky Day, but it celebrates small batch makers who dry meat with their own marinades and smoke. Drying meat is one of the oldest ways people have kept food from spoiling, long before refrigerators existed.

Jerky works because removing water stops bacteria from growing. Lean cuts are sliced thin, often against the grain for a tender chew, then seasoned and dried slowly at low heat. Fat goes rancid faster than lean meat, which is why good jerky starts with very lean cuts. Mushrooms and other plants can be made into jerky too.

Ways to celebrate

  • Make beef jerky in your oven.
  • Try a jerky from a local small maker.
  • Pack jerky for a hike or road trip.

About national days

Are national days official?

Most are not. In the United States only Congress and the president create federal holidays, and there are eleven of them. The rest are proclaimed by presidents, governors or mayors, adopted by the United Nations, or started by a club, a company or one stubborn person who kept at it.

What is the difference between a holiday and an observance?

A holiday usually means time off: federal offices and many businesses close. An observance is a date people mark without closing anything, such as World Oceans Day or National Pickle Day.

Who decides when a national day falls?

Whoever starts it. Some pick a birthday, as World Diabetes Day did with Frederick Banting's. Some pick a joke: Mad Hatter Day is October 6 because the Hatter's price tag reads 10/6. Others float, such as Thanksgiving on the fourth Thursday in November.

How do you check these dates?

We publish a day only after finding its date at a source we can name, and each page links to it. Days that failed the check are left off, even when other lists carry them. If you spot a mistake, write to us and we will fix it.
