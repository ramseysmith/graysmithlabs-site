What holiday is it on April 19?

April 19 has 2 observances to celebrate: National Garlic Day and Bicycle Day.

🧄Food

National Garlic Day

A pungent and flavorful celebration of one of the world's most beloved cooking ingredients.

🚲Fun

Bicycle Day

A day commemorating the first intentional LSD trip taken by Swiss chemist Albert Hofmann on April 19, 1943, when he rode his bicycle home.
