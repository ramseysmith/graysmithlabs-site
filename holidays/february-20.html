What holiday is it on February 20?

February 20 is World Day of Social Justice and Love Your Pet Day. It falls on a Friday in 2026 and a Saturday in 2027.

Written and illustrated by Graysmith Labs, the makers of CelebriDay.

Awareness

World Day of Social Justice

A United Nations day promoting efforts to tackle poverty, exclusion, gender inequality, unemployment, and human rights violations worldwide.

The United Nations General Assembly declared February 20 the World Day of Social Justice in 2007, and it was first observed in 2009. It asks countries and people to work toward fair chances for everyone, whatever their gender, age, race, religion, disability or income.

The day often focuses on decent work, fair pay and an end to poverty. Social justice starts close to home too, in schools, workplaces and neighborhoods where people make sure no one is left out.

Ways to celebrate

  • Support a local food bank or shelter.
  • Read about a cause you care about and take one action.
  • Shop at a local small business today.

Fun

Love Your Pet Day

A wonderful holiday dedicated to showering your beloved animal companions with extra love, treats, and attention.

Love Your Pet Day is a day to spoil the animals who share our homes. About two thirds of American households have a pet, most often a dog or a cat, with fish, birds and small animals close behind.

Pets give back plenty. Many people say their pets ease stress and loneliness, and dogs get their owners walking every day. A little extra attention, a new toy or a vet checkup is a fair trade.

Ways to celebrate

  • Take your dog on a longer walk or a new trail.
  • Make a simple homemade pet treat.
  • Donate food or blankets to a local animal shelter.
