What holiday is it on December 22?

December 22 is National Date Nut Bread Day and Forefathers' Day. It falls on a Tuesday in 2026 and a Wednesday in 2027.

Written and illustrated by Graysmith Labs, the makers of CelebriDay.

Food

National Date Nut Bread Day

A day celebrating the wonderfully moist and nutty quick bread that has been a holiday staple for generations.

Nobody is sure who started National Date Nut Bread Day. Date nut bread is a quick bread, meaning it rises with baking soda or baking powder instead of yeast. Chopped dates are often soaked in boiling water first so they soften and melt into the batter, and walnuts add crunch.

In the United States it became a classic holiday bread in the twentieth century, sometimes even sold in cans, and it is often served in slices with cream cheese. Dates bring natural sweetness and moisture, which is why the loaf stays soft for days.

Ways to celebrate

  • Bake a loaf of date nut bread.
  • Spread a slice with cream cheese.
  • Wrap a loaf as a neighbor gift.

Culture

Forefathers' Day

A New England tradition commemorating the landing of the Pilgrims at Plymouth Rock on December 22, 1620.

Forefathers Day remembers the Pilgrims' landing at Plymouth in 1620. The Old Colony Club in Plymouth first celebrated it in 1769, and the day is still marked in Plymouth with a procession and a traditional dinner that features succotash.

The date itself has a curious twist. The Pilgrims recorded the landing under the old calendar, and when converting to the modern calendar, the early celebrants added the wrong number of days. As a result, December 22 is used, though many historians place the landing on December 21.

Ways to celebrate

  • Read a short history of Plymouth Colony.
  • Cook a pot of succotash for dinner.
  • Look up your family's history online.
