What holiday is it on November 25?

November 25 is Thanksgiving and National Parfait Day. It falls on a Wednesday in 2026 and a Thursday in 2027.

Written and illustrated by Graysmith Labs, the makers of CelebriDay.

Food

Thanksgiving

A day to gather with family and friends, share a big meal, and give thanks for the good things in life.

In the fall of 1621, the Pilgrims of Plymouth Colony and about 90 Wampanoag men led by Massasoit shared a harvest feast that lasted three days. It was not called Thanksgiving at the time, and the menu likely had venison and wild fowl rather than the dinner we know now.

Days of thanks came and went for two centuries, until the writer Sarah Josepha Hale campaigned for a national holiday. President Abraham Lincoln proclaimed one in 1863, and a 1941 law fixed it on the fourth Thursday of November. For many Native Americans it is also a day of remembrance.

Ways to celebrate

  • Go around the table and share one thing you are thankful for.
  • Invite someone who would otherwise spend the day alone.
  • Take a walk together between dinner and dessert.

The date moves each year. In 2027 it falls on Thursday, November 25. After that: November 23 in 2028, November 22 in 2029, November 28 in 2030 and November 27 in 2031.

Food

National Parfait Day

A layered celebration of the beautiful and delicious parfait dessert.

Nobody is sure who first marked National Parfait Day. The parfait began in France as a frozen dessert, but in the United States the word came to mean something different: a tall glass filled with layers of ice cream, fruit, syrup and whipped cream. Today it most often means yogurt, granola and berries.

Part of the charm of a parfait is the glass itself. Clear sides show off every layer, so it is a dessert built to be looked at before it is eaten. The layers also mean each spoonful tastes a little different from the last.

Ways to celebrate

  • Layer yogurt, granola and berries in a glass.
  • Make a frozen parfait the classic French way.
  • Build a parfait bar for breakfast with family.

Same date every year. Date checked against nationaldaycalendar.com. How we check.
