What holiday is it on January 18?

January 18 is Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Winnie the Pooh Day and Thesaurus Day. It falls on a Sunday in 2026 and a Monday in 2027.

Written and illustrated by Graysmith Labs, the makers of CelebriDay.

Awareness

Martin Luther King Jr. Day

A federal holiday honoring the life and legacy of Dr. Martin Luther King Jr., civil rights leader and advocate for equality.

Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. was born on January 15, 1929, in Atlanta. A Baptist minister, he led the Montgomery bus boycott, helped organize the 1963 March on Washington, where he gave his I Have a Dream speech, and won the Nobel Peace Prize in 1964. He was assassinated in Memphis in 1968.

President Ronald Reagan signed the holiday into law in 1983, and it was first observed in 1986 on the third Monday in January. In 1994 Congress made it a national day of service: a day on, not a day off.

Ways to celebrate

  • Volunteer for a service project in your community.
  • Read or listen to the I Have a Dream speech in full.
  • Visit a civil rights museum or memorial.

The date moves each year. In 2027 it falls on Monday, January 18.
