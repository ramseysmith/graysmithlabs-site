What holiday is it on January 27?

January 27 is Holocaust Remembrance Day and Chocolate Cake Day. It falls on a Tuesday in 2026 and a Wednesday in 2027.

Awareness

Holocaust Remembrance Day

An international day of commemoration in memory of the victims of the Holocaust, observed on the anniversary of the liberation of Auschwitz.

On this day the world remembers the six million Jews murdered by Nazi Germany and its collaborators, along with Roma and Sinti people, disabled people, Polish and Soviet civilians, Soviet prisoners of war, gay men, political opponents and many others who were persecuted and killed.

Auschwitz Birkenau was the largest of the Nazi camps. About 1.1 million people were murdered there, most of them Jews. When Soviet soldiers arrived, they found about 7,000 prisoners still alive, many of them gravely ill. Survivors spent their lives telling what happened so it would never be forgotten. The camp is now a memorial and museum, and survivors' testimonies are preserved so future generations can hear them in their own words.

Ways to celebrate

  • Read or listen to a survivor's testimony.
  • Light a candle and keep a moment of silence.
  • Support a Holocaust education or memorial organization.

Food

Chocolate Cake Day

A decadent day dedicated to one of the world's most beloved desserts: the chocolate cake.

Nobody is sure who started Chocolate Cake Day. For most of history chocolate was something people drank, not something they baked with, and some early recipes called chocolate cakes were plain cakes meant to be eaten with a cup of hot chocolate.

Baking with chocolate became much easier in the 1800s. In 1828 Coenraad van Houten in the Netherlands patented a press that separated cocoa butter from the cocoa solids, which led to cocoa powder. By the late 1800s American cookbooks included cakes with chocolate in the batter. Devil's food cake, dark and moist, appeared in print around 1900, possibly named as a contrast to light, white angel food cake.

Ways to celebrate

  • Bake a chocolate cake with cocoa powder.
  • Try a flourless chocolate cake recipe.
  • Share a slice with someone having a hard week.
