Questions

How many days until Christmas?

The countdown at the top of this page shows the exact number of days, hours and minutes until Christmas in your own time zone. Christmas 2026 is Friday, December 25.

What day of the week is Christmas 2026?

Christmas Day 2026 falls on a Friday, and Christmas Eve is the Thursday before it. Christmas is always December 25, so only the weekday changes from year to year.

How many sleeps until Christmas?

The number of sleeps is the same as the number of days shown in the countdown: each night between now and Christmas Eve counts as one sleep, and the last one is the night of December 24.

When is Christmas 2027?

Christmas 2027 is Saturday, December 25.

Is Christmas a federal holiday?

Yes. Christmas Day has been a federal holiday in the United States since 1870. When December 25 falls on a Saturday, federal workers get the Friday before off, and when it falls on a Sunday, the Monday after.

What national days are in December before Christmas?

The list on this page shows every verified observance from December 1 to Christmas, such as National Ugly Sweater Day, Festivus, National Eggnog Day. The CelebriDay app names each one in a morning notification.
