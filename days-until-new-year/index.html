Questions

How many days until New Year's?

The countdown at the top of this page shows the exact number of days, hours, minutes and seconds until midnight on New Year's Day in your own time zone. New Year's Day 2027 is Friday, January 1, and New Year's Eve is Thursday, December 31, 2026.

What day of the week is New Year's Day 2027?

January 1, 2027 falls on a Friday, and New Year's Eve is the Thursday before it. New Year's Day is always January 1, so only the weekday changes from year to year.

Does the countdown use my time zone?

Yes. It counts down to midnight on January 1 on your own device's clock, so it reaches zero at the moment the new year starts where you are, not in New York or London.

When is New Year's Day 2028?

New Year's Day 2028 is Saturday, January 1, 2028.

Is New Year's Day a federal holiday?

Yes. New Year's Day is a federal holiday in the United States. When January 1 falls on a Saturday, federal workers get the Friday before off, and when it falls on a Sunday, the Monday after. New Year's Eve is not a federal holiday.

What national days are between Christmas and New Year's?

The list on this page shows every verified observance from December 26 to January 1, such as National Fruitcake Day, National Bacon Day, First Foot Day. The CelebriDay app names each one in a morning notification.
