Weird holidays in November 2026

The weirdest holidays in November 2026 include National Deviled Egg Day (Monday, November 2), National Scrapple Day (November 9), National Pickle Day (November 14), World Toilet Day (November 19), World Hello Day (November 21) and Red Planet Day (November 28). Every date on this page was checked against a public source; the full day by day list is on the November holidays page.

Fun November national days to celebrate at work

These fall on workdays or are easy to move to one, and none needs more than a snack or five minutes.

  • National Sandwich Day, Tuesday, November 3. Team lunch, or a build your own sandwich bar.
  • National Doughnut Day, Thursday, November 5. The easiest office treat of the month. (The June Doughnut Day is a different one.)
  • National Cappuccino Day, Sunday, November 8. It falls on a Sunday, so the coffee run moves to Monday the 9th.
  • World Hello Day, Saturday, November 21. On a Saturday this year, so greet a coworker you have never met on Friday the 20th.
  • Celebrate Your Unique Talent Day, Tuesday, November 24. A five minute talent share at the start of a meeting.
  • National Cake Day, Thursday, November 26. Thursday the 26th is Thanksgiving, so bring the cake on Wednesday the 25th.
  • Computer Security Day, Monday, November 30. A good Monday to update passwords and turn on two step sign in.

The big November dates

  • Daylight saving time ends, Sunday, November 1 (clocks fall back one hour).
  • Veterans Day, Wednesday, November 11.
  • Thanksgiving, Thursday, November 26, and Black Friday the next day.
  • Giving Tuesday is Tuesday, December 1 this year.

CelebriDay

CelebriDay is an iPhone and iPad app that tells you what national day it is today, every day of the year: a morning notification names today's holiday, and each of its 700 plus observances, from National Pizza Day to World Emoji Day, has a fun fact and a card to share on social media. Today and tomorrow are free in the app, and a free list of national days by month, with every date checked, is on the web at graysmithlabs.com/holidays/.

Free with ads: today and tomorrow, every holiday's fun fact, sharing and the 8 AM daily note. Premium, monthly or yearly with a free week for new subscribers, opens every other day of the calendar, favorites, choosing the note time and removes ads.
