Weird holidays in December 2026

The weirdest holidays in December 2026 include Pretend to Be a Time Traveler Day (Tuesday, December 8), National Noodle Ring Day (December 11), National Chocolate Covered Anything Day (December 16), Festivus (December 23), Make a Cut Out Snowflake Day (December 27) and Tick Tock Day (December 29). Every date on this page was checked against a public source; the full day by day list is on the December holidays page.

Fun December national days to celebrate at work

Most of these fall on workdays before the holiday break, and none needs more than a snack or five minutes.

  • National Pie Day, Tuesday, December 1. A pie potluck to open the month.
  • National Cookie Day, Friday, December 4. The easiest office treat of the month, and a natural cookie swap.
  • National Brownie Day, Tuesday, December 8. Pairs well with acting like a time traveler.
  • Christmas Card Day, Wednesday, December 9. Sign one card from the whole team for a client or another office.
  • National Cupcake Day, Tuesday, December 15. Cupcakes for everyone with a December birthday.
  • National Maple Syrup Day, Thursday, December 17. A pancake breakfast before the break.
  • Go Caroling Day, Sunday, December 20. On a Sunday this year, so the office playlist goes festive on Friday the 18th.
  • National Card Playing Day, Monday, December 28. A quiet week for a lunchtime game of cards.

The big December dates

  • Giving Tuesday, Tuesday, December 1.
  • Hanukkah begins at sundown on Friday, December 4, and ends at sundown on Saturday, December 12.
  • Winter solstice, Monday, December 21.
  • Christmas Eve, Thursday, December 24, and Christmas Day, Friday, December 25.
  • Kwanzaa begins Saturday, December 26, the same day as Boxing Day.
  • New Year's Eve, Thursday, December 31.

CelebriDay is an iPhone and iPad app that tells you what national day it is today, every day of the year: a morning notification names today's holiday, and each of its 700 plus observances, from National Pizza Day to World Emoji Day, has a fun fact and a card to share on social media.
