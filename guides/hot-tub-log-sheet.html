Hot tub log sheet (free printable PDF)

Download the hot tub log sheet (PDF): one page to write down each test with the target ranges at the top, and one page of maintenance jobs to tick off. It prints on US letter paper, uses little ink and needs no email.

Updated November 25, 2026 by Graysmith Labs

What is on the log sheet

Page one has 26 rows, about two months of tests at three a week. Each column shows the range to aim for.

ColumnTargetHow often
Free chlorine3 to 10 ppm2 to 3 times a week
Bromine, if you use it instead4 to 8 ppm2 to 3 times a week
pH7.2 to 7.82 to 3 times a week
Total alkalinity80 to 120 ppmOnce a week
Calcium hardness150 to 400 ppmOnce a month and after a refill
Water temperatureYour settingWhen you test
Added, and how muchProduct and amountEvery time you add something

Use one row per test and leave a box blank when you did not test it. The label on your chemicals comes first when it disagrees with a range here.

The maintenance checklist on page two

  • Two to three times a week: test sanitizer and pH, and look at the water.
  • Every week: test alkalinity, wipe the waterline and pillows.
  • Every two weeks: rinse the filter, pleat by pleat.
  • Every month: test calcium hardness, wipe the cover inside and out.
  • Every three to four months: drain, clean and refill, then test daily for the first week.

The reasons behind each interval are in how often to test hot tub water.

How to read your own log

  • Alkalinity falls first, then pH swings: raise alkalinity before you chase pH.
  • pH climbs a little after every soak: jets add air, and air raises pH. See hot tub pH too high.
  • Sanitizer is gone by the next test each time: you are adding too little for the number of soakers, or the water is old.
  • You keep adding more of everything to hold the same numbers: count the days since the last refill, the water may be due.

Write down what you added as well as the reading. The log is only useful when you can see what each dose did.

Working out the amounts

A log tells you what is off; it does not tell you how much to add. The free hot tub calculator takes your tub size and readings and gives the amounts in order: alkalinity, pH, calcium, then sanitizer.

Keeping the log on your phone instead

Paper works just as well for many people, and the PDF is free either way.

Questions

Is the hot tub log sheet free to copy?

Yes. Print as many as you like for your home, rental or front desk. No email or account is needed.

What should I write in a hot tub log?

The date, chlorine or bromine, pH, alkalinity, calcium when you test it, the water temperature, and the name and amount of anything you added.

How many rows do I need?

At three tests a week, the 26 rows on one sheet last about two months.

Can I use it for a rental hot tub?

Yes, as a home record between guests. Public and commercial spas have their own rules on what to record and how often, so use the form your health department asks for.

Can I use it with bromine or salt?

Yes. Use the bromine range for bromine. Most salt systems make chlorine, so use the chlorine range, or the range your system's manual gives.
