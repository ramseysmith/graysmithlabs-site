How to sign one document for free without DocuSign

If someone sent you a DocuSign link, you can sign it for free without creating an account: open the email, tap Review Document and follow the prompts. If you only have a PDF or a paper form to sign yourself, you do not need DocuSign at all: sign it with Markup in the iPhone Files app, or with a signing app such as SignSnap, which needs no account and keeps the document on your phone.

Updated October 28, 2026 by Graysmith Labs

First: did someone send you a DocuSign link?

Signing a document someone sent you through DocuSign is always free. The sender pays; you do not need an account or the app.

  1. Open the email from DocuSign and tap Review Document.
  2. Agree to use electronic records and signatures when it asks.
  3. Tap each yellow tag to place your signature, initials or date. The first time, DocuSign asks you to adopt a signature: pick a style or draw one.
  4. Tap Finish. You can download a copy without signing up.

Only make an account if you want to send documents to other people for signature. That is the part DocuSign charges for.

Signing a PDF yourself: Markup in Files (free, built in)

For a PDF that is already on your iPhone, the Files app signs it with nothing to install.

  1. In Mail, tap the attachment, tap the share button and choose Save to Files.
  2. Open the PDF in Files and tap the Markup button (a pen tip inside a circle).
  3. Tap the plus button, choose Add Signature, sign with your finger and tap Done.
  4. Drag the signature onto the line, resize it, then tap Done and share the PDF back.

The full step by step version is in how to sign a PDF on iPhone.

Paper forms and frequent signing: SignSnap

SignSnap is an iPhone and iPad app from Graysmith Labs made only for signing your own documents. It suits paper forms, and people who sign often and want the date stamped beside the signature.

  1. Scan the page with the camera (the scanner finds the edges and straightens it) or import the PDF from Files or Photos.
  2. Draw your signature with your finger, or type your name and pick a script font. It is saved for next time.
  3. Place it on the line, pinch to resize and tap Date to stamp today's date.
  4. Share the signed PDF to Mail, Messages or AirDrop.

No account and no uploads. The first 2 signed documents are free, then one short ad unlocks one more signing; Premium (weekly, monthly or yearly, with a free week for new subscribers) removes the limit and the ads.

Is it legally valid without DocuSign?

In the United States, the ESIGN Act and the state laws based on UETA generally treat an electronic signature as valid whatever tool made it, as long as both sides agree to sign electronically. DocuSign adds an audit trail of who signed and when, which matters most for high value contracts.

Some documents need more, such as wills, certain court papers and anything that must be notarized. When unsure, ask whoever sent the document whether a signed PDF is acceptable. This is general information, not legal advice.

Questions

Can I sign a DocuSign document without an account?

Yes. Signing a document someone sent you through DocuSign is free and needs no account: open the email, tap Review Document, place your signature and tap Finish.

What is a free alternative to DocuSign for one document?

To sign a PDF yourself, use Markup in the iPhone Files app, which is built in, or a signing app such as SignSnap, whose first 2 signed documents are free with no account.

Do I need DocuSign to sign a PDF on my phone?

No. Any PDF can be signed with Markup in the Files app or with a signing app, then emailed back.

Can SignSnap send a document to someone else to sign?

No. SignSnap is for signing your own documents. To collect signatures from other people, use a service built for that, such as DocuSign.
