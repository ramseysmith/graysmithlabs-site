Sleep sounds that work offline on a plane

Yes: a sleep sounds app plays on a plane as long as its sounds are stored on your phone, so pick one whose sounds ship inside the app (Drift, for example) or download your sounds before you board, and test it in airplane mode at home. Test it in airplane mode before you fly

Many sleep apps stream their sounds or keep most of them online. Find out on the ground, not at 35,000 feet.

  1. Open Control Center and turn on Airplane Mode. Turn WiFi off too, because planes often let you turn it back on.
  2. Open your sleep app and play each sound you plan to use.
  3. Lock the screen and wait a minute. The sound should keep playing.
  4. Set the sleep timer and make sure it still counts down offline.
  5. If a sound will not play, it is streamed. Download it inside the app, or use an app whose sounds are built in.

Which sounds cover engine noise best

Cabin noise is a steady low roar, so a steady sound with plenty of low end covers it best. Fan and a deep white noise (often called brown noise) blend into the engine instead of fighting it. Rain and ocean change over time, which some people find more relaxing but which masks less.

Keep the volume low. Headphones on a plane are easy to turn up too far, and a sound only needs to be slightly louder than the noise you are covering.

Set up before boarding

  • Charge your phone and headphones, or pack a short cable.
  • Pick one sound and a sleep timer of about an hour so the app stops on its own.
  • Wired headphones or earbuds that fit well block more noise than loose ones. Noise cancelling headphones plus a quiet sound work better than either alone.
  • Turn on Do Not Disturb or a Sleep focus so notifications do not wake you when you land.

Questions

Do sleep sound apps work in airplane mode?

Only if the sounds are stored on your phone. Apps that stream need you to download sounds first. Drift's sounds ship inside the app, so it works in airplane mode with nothing to prepare.

What is the best sound to sleep on a plane?

A steady sound with low tones, such as fan or a deep white noise, blends into engine noise better than changing sounds like waves.

Will the sound keep playing when my iPhone locks?

In apps that support background audio, yes. Test it at home by locking the screen for a minute before you rely on it in the air.

Is white noise safe to listen to on a long flight?

Keep the volume at the lowest level that covers the cabin noise and use a sleep timer so it does not play all flight.
