Guides

Short how to guides for everyday tasks on your phone. Each one shows the free built in way first, then where one of our apps saves time.

  • How to sign a PDF on iPhoneHow to sign a PDF on iPhone for free: use Markup in the Files app, or scan and sign with SignSnap. Step by step, no printer and no account.
  • How to fill and sign a lease on your phoneFill in and sign a rental lease on iPhone: get it as a PDF, type into the blanks, initial and sign each page, and send it back. No printer needed.
  • Cloudy hot tub water: why it happens and how to fix itWhy is my hot tub water cloudy? Usually low sanitizer, high pH or alkalinity, or a dirty filter. Test, balance, shock and rinse the filter, in that order.
  • How to lower hot tub alkalinityLower hot tub alkalinity: add pH decreaser until pH is about 7.2, then run the jets with the cover off so pH rises. Repeat daily until 80 to 120 ppm.
  • How much chlorine to add to a hot tubHow much chlorine to add to a hot tub: about 1¾ tsp of dichlor or 2½ fl oz of 6% bleach raises a 400 gallon tub by 3 ppm. Keep it at 3 to 10 ppm.
  • Hot tub pH too high? How to lower itHot tub pH too high: check alkalinity first, then add pH decreaser with the pump on. About 2¾ tsp takes a 400 gallon tub from pH 8.0 to 7.5.
  • How often to test hot tub waterTest hot tub sanitizer and pH two to three times a week and before every soak after a busy day, alkalinity weekly, calcium monthly. Full schedule inside.
  • Bromine vs chlorine for a hot tubBromine holds steadier in hot water and smells less; chlorine costs less and works faster. Levels, cost, smell, sunlight and how to switch safely.
  • Sleep sounds that work offline on a planeYes, you can play sleep sounds offline on a plane. Use an app whose sounds ship inside it, test in airplane mode before you fly, and set a sleep timer.
  • Brown noise for sleep: what it is and how to use itBrown noise is a deep, soft rumble with more low tones than white noise. How it differs, who it suits, and how to play it free all night on iPhone.
  • How to improve your reaction timeHow to improve reaction time: short daily practice on fast decisions, enough sleep and fewer distractions. Test a baseline, train, retest weekly.
  • How to sign one document for free without DocuSignNeed to sign just one document? Three free ways to sign a PDF on iPhone with no DocuSign account or subscription, and when DocuSign still fits.
