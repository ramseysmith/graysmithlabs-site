How to sign a PDF on iPhone

You can sign a PDF on iPhone for free with Markup, which is built into the Files app: open the PDF, tap the Markup button, tap the plus button, choose Add Signature and drag it onto the line. If the document is on paper, or you sign things often, a signing app such as SignSnap scans the page with the camera, keeps your signature ready and adds the date in one tap.

Updated October 28, 2026 by Graysmith Labs

Method 1: Markup in the Files app (built in, free)

Every iPhone can do this without installing anything. It works best when the PDF is already on your phone, for example as an email attachment.

  1. Save the PDF to Files. In Mail, tap the attachment, tap the share button and choose Save to Files.
  2. Open the Files app, find the PDF and tap it to open it.
  3. Tap the Markup button (a pen tip inside a circle).
  4. Tap the plus button, then Add Signature. Sign with your finger and tap Done. iPhone remembers it for next time.
  5. Drag the signature onto the line and drag its corners to make it fit.
  6. Tap Done. Files saves the signature into that PDF. Tap the share button to send it back by Mail or Messages.

Method 2: SignSnap (paper documents and frequent signing)

SignSnap is a free iPhone and iPad app from Graysmith Labs made only for signing. It helps most when you were handed paper, when you sign often, or when you want the date next to your signature.

  1. Open SignSnap and import the PDF from the Files app (save an email attachment to Files first) or Photos, or scan a paper page with the camera. The scanner finds the edges and straightens the page.
  2. Create your signature once: draw it with your finger, or type your name in a script font. You can save your initials the same way; each signing places one mark plus the date.
  3. Place it on the line and pinch to resize it. Tap Date to stamp today's date beside it.
  4. Share the signed PDF to Mail, Messages, AirDrop or Files.

No account is needed and documents stay on your iPhone. The first 2 signed documents are free; after that Premium is weekly, monthly or yearly, and new subscribers get a free week.

Is a signature made on an iPhone legally valid?

In the United States, the federal ESIGN Act and the state laws based on UETA generally give electronic signatures the same standing as ink for most everyday contracts, including leases, offer letters and permission slips. Some documents are excluded or need more, such as wills, certain court papers and anything that must be notarized.

If you are not sure, ask whoever sent the document whether a signed PDF is acceptable. This guide is general information, not legal advice.

Questions

Can I sign a PDF on iPhone without downloading an app?

Yes. Open the PDF in the Files app, tap the Markup button, tap the plus button and choose Add Signature. Drag the signature onto the line and tap Done.

How do I sign a PDF that someone emailed me?

Tap the attachment in Mail, tap the share button and choose Save to Files. Then sign it with Markup in Files, or import it into a signing app such as SignSnap, and share the signed PDF back as a reply.

How do I sign a paper document with my iPhone?

Scan it first. SignSnap has a scanner built in that finds the page edges, so you can scan, sign and send a one page form in one app. For several pages, the Notes app can scan them into one PDF.

Can I add the date next to my signature?

In Markup, tap the plus button, choose Add Text and type the date. In SignSnap, tap Date while placing your signature to stamp today's date.

Does SignSnap upload my documents?

No. SignSnap needs no account and keeps documents on your iPhone. A file leaves the phone only when you share it.
