How do I put affirmations on my iPhone lock screen?

The free way is to make a wallpaper with your affirmation on it and set it as your Lock Screen, or to add a daily repeating reminder whose text is your affirmation so it appears on the Lock Screen each morning. If you want a different affirmation through the day without making images, add a Lock Screen widget from an affirmations app such as SayBright (its widgets come with Premium).

Updated November 4, 2026 by Graysmith Labs

Free method 1: an affirmation wallpaper

This takes about two minutes and needs no app.

  1. Write your affirmation in large text: in Notes, in a photo editor, or on paper you photograph. Short lines read best, for example I am calm and capable.
  2. Take a screenshot or a photo of it and crop it in Photos so the words sit in the middle of the frame, below where the clock will be.
  3. Open Settings, tap Wallpaper, then Add New Wallpaper, and choose Photos.
  4. Pick your image, move it so the clock does not cover the words, and tap Add.
  5. Choose Set as Wallpaper Pair, or Customize Home Screen if you want a different home screen.

Free method 2: a daily reminder that says it for you

A notification shows on the Lock Screen, so a repeating reminder puts the words in front of you at the time you choose.

  1. Open the Reminders app and tap New Reminder.
  2. Type your affirmation as the reminder's title.
  3. Tap the details button, turn on Date and Time, and pick the time you want to see it.
  4. Tap Repeat and choose Daily, then tap Done.
  5. Each day the affirmation appears on your Lock Screen. Mark it done after you read it out loud, and it comes back tomorrow.

With an app: a Lock Screen widget

A wallpaper says the same thing every day. An affirmations app widget changes through the day, so the words do not fade into the background. To add one, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, tap Lock Screen, tap the area below the clock and pick the app's widget.

SayBright is one option: its free plan gives five affirmations a day, the whole Confidence category, a daily reminder and streaks, and Premium ($0.99 a month or $9.99 a year) adds home screen and Lock Screen widgets, every category and recordings in your own voice. Other affirmation apps such as I am and ThinkUp also offer widgets, so pick the one whose words sound like you.

Make it stick

  • Keep it short and in the present tense: I handle hard things, not I will be less anxious.
  • Read it out loud once when you first see it. Saying it is what turns a wallpaper into a habit.
  • Change it every week or two so your eyes do not skip over it.
  • Pair it with something you already do, like the first time you unlock your phone in the morning.

Questions

How do I put affirmations on my iPhone lock screen for free?

Make an image with your affirmation, then open Settings, Wallpaper, Add New Wallpaper, Photos, and set it as your Lock Screen. Or create a daily repeating reminder in Reminders whose title is your affirmation.

Is there an affirmation widget for the iPhone lock screen?

Yes. Several affirmation apps offer Lock Screen widgets. SayBright's home screen and Lock Screen widgets come with Premium, $0.99 a month or $9.99 a year, and its free plan gives five affirmations a day.

How do I add a widget to my iPhone lock screen?

Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, tap Lock Screen, then tap the area below the clock and choose a widget. Tap Done to save.

Can I record affirmations in my own voice?

Yes, with SayBright Premium you can record any affirmation in your own voice, and the recordings stay on your phone.
