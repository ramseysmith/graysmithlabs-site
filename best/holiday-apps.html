  1. Best overall, and best for sharing

    CelebriDay shows one holiday per screen with its own drawn illustration and a fun fact, and turns it into a square picture for messages or a 9:16 picture for Stories in one tap.     • Original art for every one of 700 plus days
    • Share pictures sized for posts and Stories
    • Birthday national days for family and friends
    • Today and tomorrow free, forever

    Pick something else if: No home screen widget yet.

  2. Best for sourced detail

    Checkiday

    Checkiday writes detailed pages for each observance with where it came from, and ships updates several times a month. It has a widget and a well known website.

    • Detailed origins for each day
    • Widget and frequent updates

    Pick something else if: Text first, with less to share as a picture.

  3. Most popular, with a widget

    National Holiday Today

    Rated 4.6 from thousands of reviews, it is a long running special day calendar with a widget and sharing built in.

    • Years of ratings and polish
    • Home screen widget

    Pick something else if: Stock style imagery rather than art made for each day.

  4. Best for the biggest list

    National Day: Calendar 2026

    This app leads with more than 1,000 holidays and fun days and pitches them as conversation starters.

    • Very large list
    • Photo led design

    Pick something else if: A smaller, newer app with few ratings so far.

  5. Best built in option for public holidays

    Apple Calendar

    The Calendar app already on your iPhone can show public holidays like Thanksgiving and Memorial Day. It does not include national days such as National Coffee Day.

    • Free and already installed

    Pick something else if: Official holidays only, no fun national days.

Our pick: CelebriDay

CelebriDay is an iPhone and iPad app that tells you what national day it is today, every day of the year. Each of its 700 plus observances, from National Pizza Day to World Emoji Day, has its own illustration, a fun fact and a picture to share as a square post or a 9:16 story, and a morning note names the day with its picture. A People tab shows the national day each friend or family member was born on, with a birthday card ready to send. Today and tomorrow are free in the app, and a free list of national days by month, with every date checked, is on the web at graysmithlabs.com/holidays/.

Free with ads: today and tomorrow, every holiday's art and fun fact, square and story share pictures, the morning note with the day's picture, your own birthday holiday and up to three people in People. Premium, monthly or yearly with a free week for new subscribers, opens every other day of the calendar, unlimited people with a note the day before each birthday, the full idea lists for big holidays such as Thanksgiving and Christmas, favorites, choosing the note time, and removes ads.

Questions

What app tells you what national day it is?

CelebriDay tells you what national day it is today with its own illustration, a fun fact and a picture to share, and sends a morning note so you see it first thing. Checkiday and National Holiday Today are good alternatives.

Is there a free app for national days?

Yes. CelebriDay's today and tomorrow are free with ads, including the art, fun fact and share pictures. You can also browse every day by month on our free national day calendar.

What national day is my birthday?

CelebriDay's People tab shows the national day you were born on, and the one for each friend or family member you add.

Does the iPhone Calendar show national days?

No. Apple Calendar shows public holidays like Thanksgiving, not fun observances like National Donut Day. You need an app such as CelebriDay for those.
