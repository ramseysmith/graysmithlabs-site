Apps to sign documents on iPhone, compared

For a PDF already on your phone, the built in Markup tool is free and enough. If someone sent you a DocuSign request, sign it there for free. SignSnap is for paper forms and repeat signing, Adobe Fill & Sign for form fields, and Signeasy for sending a few requests.

Updated November 25, 2026 by Graysmith Labs, Ramsey Graysmith’s studio

We make one of these. SignSnap is our app, listed first so you know which it is. This is a comparison, not a ranking. Details on the others come from their App Store listings and reviews. How we choose apps.

What we looked for

  • Paper and PDF. Leases and permission slips often arrive on paper, not as a file.
  • No account. Signing one document should not mean a new login.
  • Privacy. Whether your documents leave your phone.
  • Speed. Saved signature, initials and the date in a tap or two.

5 ways to do it, and who each suits

  1. Best for paper forms and repeat signing · our app

    SignSnap

    SignSnap does one job well. Scan a page with the camera (it finds the edges and straightens it) or import a PDF from Files or Photos, drop in the signature you drew or typed once, pinch to size it, tap to stamp the date, and share the PDF to Mail, Messages or AirDrop. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account. The first 2 signed documents are free, a short ad unlocks another, and Premium removes the limit.

    • Scans paper forms, not just PDFs
    • No account and no cloud uploads
    • Saved signature, initials and a date stamp

    Pick something else if: Made for signing yourself; it does not send documents out for others to sign.

    Get SignSnap on the App Store About SignSnap

  2. Best free option for a PDF you already have

    Markup (built into iPhone)

    Open a PDF in the Files app, tap Markup, then the plus button and Add Signature. It is free and saves your signature, but scanning paper and adding a date take extra steps.

    • Free and already installed

    Pick something else if: No date stamp, and paper needs a separate scan first.

  3. Best for filling in form fields

    Adobe Fill & Sign

    Adobe's free app is good at typing into form fields and signing. It works best when you are happy to use an Adobe account.

    • Strong form filling
    • Free to use

    Pick something else if: Saving a plain local copy is less direct than in simpler apps.

  4. Best when someone sends you a DocuSign request

    DocuSign

    Signing documents other people send you through DocuSign is free. Sending documents for others to sign is where the paid plans start.

    • Legally robust audit trail
    • Free to sign what you are sent

    Pick something else if: Overkill, and an account, for signing your own PDF.

  5. Best for occasional sending

    Signeasy

    Signeasy lets you sign and send documents, with the first few documents free before a paid plan.

    • Sign and request signatures in one app

    Pick something else if: Account required and a limited free tier.

About SignSnap, our app

SignSnap is a free iPhone and iPad app to sign a PDF or scan and sign a paper form, such as a lease, offer letter or permission slip, with no account and no uploads: draw or type your signature, place it on the line, stamp the date and share the signed PDF. It is a simple alternative to DocuSign when you only need to sign one document yourself.

Free for the first 2 signed documents, then one rewarded ad unlocks one more signing. Premium is weekly, monthly or yearly with no ads and no limit, with a free week for new subscribers.

Questions

What is the best free app to sign documents on iPhone?

For a PDF already on your phone, Markup in the Files app is free. For paper forms, or to add the date and reuse your signature, SignSnap is free for the first 2 documents with no account.

Can I sign a paper document with my iPhone?

Yes. SignSnap scans the paper page with your camera, straightens it, and lets you sign it and send it back as a PDF. See our guide to signing a lease on your phone.

Is an iPhone signature legally binding?

In the United States, electronic signatures are generally valid under the ESIGN Act for most everyday documents such as leases and permission slips. Check with the person asking for the signature if you are unsure.

Do I need DocuSign to sign a PDF?

No. You only need DocuSign when someone sends you a DocuSign request. To sign your own PDF, use Markup or SignSnap. Our free signature maker also makes a signature image in the browser.
