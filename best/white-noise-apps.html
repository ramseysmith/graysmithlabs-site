App comparisons  /  White noise

White noise apps for iPhone, compared

Pick Drift for free sounds that play all night offline, White Noise Lite or Rain Rain for the biggest free catalogs and mixing, Dark Noise for design, and Calm only if you also want guided sleep stories. The free tiers differ far more than the sounds do, so check what stops at night.

Updated November 25, 2026 by Graysmith Labs

We make one of these. Drift is our app, listed first so you know which it is. This is a comparison, not a ranking. Details on the others come from their App Store listings and reviews. How we choose apps.

What we looked for

  • Free tier that works all night. No two hour caps and no pop ups once the lights are off.
  • Offline and locked screen playback. A sleep app that stops on a plane or when the phone locks fails at its one job.
  • Sound quality and loops. Noise should sound continuous, without an audible seam every few seconds.
  • Fair price. What Premium costs a year, and what you lose without it.

6 ways to do it, and who each suits

  1. Best for free sounds offline · our app

    Drift

    Drift is built for one thing: putting a steady sound on and falling asleep. White noise, rain, ocean, stream, fan and a 432 Hz tone are free, every sound ships inside the app so it works in airplane mode, and a sleep timer can fade the sound out as it ends. Premium adds pink noise, green noise, thunder, forest, fire, wind, cafe, more tones and Alpha, Theta and Delta binaural beats for $1.99 a month, a fraction of the big sleep apps. It is on iPhone, iPad and Android.

    • Six good sounds free, with no time cap
    • Works offline and with the screen locked
    • No account needed
    • Premium is $1.99 a month with a free first week

    Pick something else if: No mixer yet: you play one sound at a time.

  2. Best for every noise color and a bedside clock

    White Noise Lite

    TMSOFT's app has been around for about fifteen years and carries hundreds of thousands of ratings. The free version has more than 50 sounds, including white, pink, brown, blue and violet noise, plus a mix pad, alarms and a clock face.

    • Very large free catalog
    • Mixing and fading alarms

    Pick something else if: Free with ads; removing them means buying a separate paid app.

  3. Best free catalog for mixing

    Rain Rain Sleep Sounds

    Rain Rain gives away more than 50 sounds with no time limit and only a small banner, and lets you blend them with a mixer and a fade timer. People with tinnitus like its oscillator and soften filter.

    • Generous free tier with no time limit
    • Mixer, timer and a Lock Screen widget

    Pick something else if: The interface is busy next to simpler apps.

  4. Best for building your own soundscape

    BetterSleep

    Formerly Relax Melodies, BetterSleep has more than 300 sounds, per sound volume mixing, a sleep tracker and bedtime reminders. Its mixer is the one reviewers praise most.

    • Huge library and a strong mixer
    • Sleep tracking built in

    Pick something else if: Much of the library is locked, and reviews mention recurring charges.

  5. Best design and iPhone integration

    Dark Noise

    A small team app focused on noise colors, with widgets, Live Activities, a Control Center control and a fade timer. Pro comes as monthly, yearly or lifetime.

    • Beautiful, ad free design
    • Lifetime option

    Pick something else if: The free tier is thin compared with the apps above.

  6. Best if you want sleep stories, not just noise

    Calm

    Calm is a full meditation and sleep subscription with narrated sleep stories, music and soundscapes. Noise is a small part of what you pay for, and it asks for an account and pushes a yearly plan early.

    • Famous narrators and sleep stories
    • Meditation courses included

    Pick something else if: One of the most expensive choices if all you want is white noise.

About Drift, our app

Drift is a free white noise and sleep sounds app for iPhone, iPad and Android that works offline, even on a plane: white noise, brown noise, rain, ocean, stream, fan and a 432 Hz tone are free, with a sleep timer and sound that keeps playing with the screen locked. It is a free alternative to Calm for masking snoring or traffic, and Premium at $1.99 a month, with a free first week, adds pink noise, green noise, thunder, forest, night, fire, wind, cafe, 174 Hz and 528 Hz tones and Alpha, Theta and Delta binaural beats, and removes ads.

Free with seven sounds (including white and brown noise) and ads. Premium is $1.99 a month and unlocks all twenty sounds with no ads; eligible new subscribers get a free week. Premium sounds can be previewed for 30 seconds a day.

Questions

What is the best free white noise app for iPhone?

Drift is a good free choice: white noise, rain, ocean, stream, fan and a 432 Hz tone are free with no time cap, and it plays offline with the screen locked. White Noise Lite and Rain Rain have larger free catalogs if you want to mix sounds.

Do white noise apps work in airplane mode?

Drift, White Noise Lite and Rain Rain keep their sounds on the phone, so they play in airplane mode. Streaming apps like Calm need each sound downloaded first. Test in airplane mode at home before a trip.

Is white noise or pink noise better for sleep?

Both mask sounds such as traffic and snoring. Pink noise is softer and deeper, which many people find easier to sleep with. Try each for a few nights; Drift has white noise free and pink noise in Premium.

Is there a white noise app with no subscription?

White Noise Lite is free with ads and sells a one time upgrade as a separate app. Drift's free sounds need no subscription at all; Premium is optional.

Can I try white noise without an app?

Yes. Our free white noise generator plays white, pink and brown noise in the browser.
