Affirmation apps for iPhone, compared

Pick SayBright for a short daily habit at a low price, I am for the biggest following, Motivation if you want quotes, and ThinkUp if recording your own voice is the point. All of them can put words on your Lock Screen or Home Screen; what differs is how much is free.

Updated November 25, 2026 by Graysmith Labs, Ramsey Graysmith’s studio

We make one of these. SayBright is our app, listed first so you know which it is. This is a comparison, not a ranking. Details on the others come from their App Store listings and reviews. How we choose apps.

What we looked for

  • Habit first. Reminders and streaks that bring you back each day.
  • Your topics. Confidence, gratitude, self love: affirmations that fit you.
  • Seen through the day. Widgets on the Lock Screen and Home Screen.
  • Price. What a year costs.

4 ways to do it, and who each suits

  1. Best for a small daily habit · our app

    SayBright

    SayBright gets you saying your first affirmation in under a minute, then gives you five a day from the topics you pick, out of 550 in ten categories, with the whole Confidence category free. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets are free, and so is the Sunrise, which puts today's five on your Lock Screen. A reminder at the time you choose and streak milestones from 7 to 365 days keep the habit going, and you can write your own. Premium, $0.99 a month or $9.99 a year, opens every category, widgets that change all day, and recordings in your own voice.

    • Five free affirmations a day and Confidence fully free
    • Free widgets, streaks and a daily reminder
    • Premium for $9.99 a year

    Pick something else if: Free widgets show one affirmation a day; a widget that changes all day is Premium.

  2. Most popular, with free widgets

    I am

    The most popular affirmation app, with hundreds of thousands of ratings, leads with Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets and updates almost every week.

    • Free widgets
    • Huge library

    Pick something else if: A pricier subscription for the full library.

  3. Best for quotes

    Motivation

    From the same playbook as I am, with inspirational quotes and widgets, and around a million ratings.

    • Large quote library
    • Widgets

    Pick something else if: More quotes than personal affirmations.

  4. Best for recording your own voice

    ThinkUp

    ThinkUp built its name on recording affirmations in your own voice over music, backed by expert endorsements.

    • Voice recording with music

    Pick something else if: The free version is limited.

About SayBright, our app

SayBright is a daily affirmations app for iPhone that helps you start and keep a daily affirmation habit: five free affirmations a day picked from the topics you choose, free widgets, a Lock Screen sunrise that rises as you say them, streaks, and a free seasonal pack each month it runs, from 550 affirmations that all start with I am, in ten categories such as confidence, gratitude and self love. No subscription is needed for the free plan. Premium ($0.99 a month or $9.99 a year) adds every category with no daily limit, widgets that change all day, affirmations recorded in your own voice, a weekly streak save and no ads.

Free plan: five affirmations a day, the full Confidence category, previews of the other nine categories, the free seasonal pack, the Sunrise, widgets, streaks, reminders, share cards and your own written affirmations. Premium is $0.99 a month or $9.99 a year with a free week for eligible new subscribers, and unlocks every category with no daily limit, widgets that change all day, voice recordings, a streak save for one missed day a week and no ads.

Questions

What is the best free affirmation app?

SayBright gives five free affirmations a day, the whole Confidence category, widgets, streaks and reminders. I am, which has the biggest following, also has free widgets.

How do I put affirmations on my iPhone Lock Screen?

Add an affirmation app's widget from the Lock Screen editor. Our guide on affirmations on the iPhone Lock Screen walks through it.

Do affirmation apps work?

Affirmations work best as a short daily habit, which is why reminders and streaks matter more than the size of the library. Try today's affirmation of the day.
