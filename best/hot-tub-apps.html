Hot tub water care apps, compared

Pick TubTender if you have one hot tub and want doses from your own bottles, Pool Pilot if you also have a pool, and TubTest to learn testing. The label on the bottle and a dosage chart still work with no app at all.

Updated November 25, 2026 by Graysmith Labs, Ramsey Graysmith’s studio

We make one of these. TubTender is our app, listed first so you know which it is. This is a comparison, not a ranking. Details on the others come from their App Store listings and reviews. How we choose apps.

What we looked for

  • Doses for your products. Amounts in your own bottles, not a generic chemical.
  • Built for spas. Bromine and chlorine hot tubs, not pool sized math.
  • Order and wait times. What to add first and when you can get in.
  • Free use and privacy. What you get without paying or making an account.

4 ways to do it, and who each suits

  1. Best for a single hot tub · our app

    TubTender

    TubTender is made only for hot tubs. Tap the strip colors instead of typing numbers, scan your chemical labels once so it knows their strength, and get exact amounts for your tub, step by step with wait times, ending in a safe to soak verdict and countdown. Free guides fix cloudy and foamy water. Premium adds trend charts, full history and every fix it guide.

    • Amounts in the products you own
    • Right order, wait times and safe to soak
    • Free plan, no account, data stays on your iPhone

    Pick something else if: iPhone only for now, and made for hot tubs rather than pools.

    Get TubTender on the App Store About TubTender

  2. Best if you have a pool and a spa

    Pool Pilot

    Pool Pilot covers pools and spas, with free manual testing and paid photo scanning of test strips.

    • Pools and spas in one app
    • Free manual entry

    Pick something else if: Strip scanning is a paid unlock.

  3. Best for learning to test

    TubTest

    TubTest walks you through testing your water step by step and keeps the data on your device.

    • Clear testing walkthrough
    • Data stays local

    Pick something else if: Less help working out exact doses.

  4. The no app way

    The label and a dosage chart

    Every chemical bottle has a dosing table on the back. It works, but you do the math for your gallons and remember the order yourself.

    • Free

    Pick something else if: Easy to over dose, and no reminders.

About TubTender, our app

TubTender is a hot tub water care and chemical calculator app for iPhone, for bromine or chlorine tubs: tap the colors on your test strip, scan the labels of the chemicals you own, and it shows how much of each to add for your tub size, in the right order, with a countdown until the water is safe to soak. The test and dose plan is free with no account, and it is on the App Store.

Free test, dose plan, test reminders, the refill, cloudy and foamy water guides and your last 5 tests. Premium monthly or yearly adds trend charts, full history, every fix it guide, care reminders and more than one tub, with a free week to try it.

Questions

Is there an app that tells you how much chemical to put in a hot tub?

Yes. TubTender reads your test strip colors, knows your tub size and the strength of your own bottles, and gives exact amounts in order. Our free hot tub chemical calculator does the same math in the browser.

Can an app read hot tub test strips?

TubTender has you tap the matching color for each pad, which avoids lighting errors from photos. Pool Pilot offers paid photo scanning.

Why is my hot tub cloudy?

Usually low sanitizer, high pH or alkalinity, or a dirty filter. Our guide to cloudy hot tub water goes through the fixes in order.
