The 6 best invoices apps in 2026

  1. Best overall for one person businesses

    Tallyhand

    Type or dictate something like "mowed and edged the Parkers' lawn, two hours at 45" and Tallyhand turns it into line items and a total on your iPhone. Share a PDF invoice, estimate or receipt by text, email or AirDrop with your payment instructions and a Pay online link, turn an estimate into an invoice in one tap, and get a reminder on the due date with a friendly message to send. If the price ever changes, subscribers keep the price they signed up at.

    • One sentence becomes an invoice
    • Due date and overdue reminders
    • No account; first 3 documents free with every feature
    • Your price never rises while you stay subscribed

    Pick something else if: No card processing built in: the Pay online link goes to your own processor.

    Get Tallyhand on the App Store About Tallyhand

  2. Best for read receipts

    Invoice Simple

    The category leader by ratings, with a release about every two weeks, job photos on invoices and a notice when your client opens the invoice.

    • Knows when the client viewed it
    • Photo attachments

    Pick something else if: Recent reviews complain about price rises and plan caps.

  3. Fastest growing all rounder

    Invoice Fly

    Weekly releases, receipt scanning and generators for every document type.

    • Feature rich
    • Very active development

    Pick something else if: Weekly billing options that some reviewers dislike.

  4. Best for card payments

    Invoice Maker by Tofu

    Card payments by link or QR code, pay in 4, automatic overdue reminders and viewed status.

    • Payments on every invoice
    • Automatic reminders

    Pick something else if: Paid plans needed for the payment features.

  5. Best for contractors with crews

    Joist

    Joist owns the contractor niche with estimates, payments and financing for bigger jobs.

    • Built for contractors
    • Customer financing

    Pick something else if: Recent reviews complain about features moving to higher tiers.

  6. Easiest for beginners

    Easy Invoice Maker

    Starts you straight in a document with no pressure paywall and a small free allowance each year.

    • Very simple start

    Pick something else if: The free plan is only a handful of documents a year.

Our pick: Tallyhand

Tallyhand is an iPhone invoice, estimate and receipt maker for one person service businesses such as lawn care, house cleaning and handyman work: you type or dictate the job in one sentence, it builds the line items and total, shares a professional PDF, and reminds you on the due date and when a client is late. The first 3 documents are free with no account, and it is on the App Store.

First 3 invoices or estimates free with every feature. Premium monthly or yearly, with a free week to try it, adds unlimited documents, your logo on every PDF and income reports with a CSV export. If the price ever changes, existing subscribers keep the price they signed up at for as long as they stay subscribed.

Questions

What is the easiest invoice app for iPhone?

Tallyhand is the fastest: you describe the job in one sentence and it fills in the invoice. Easy Invoice Maker is also simple to start.

Is there a free invoice app with no account?

Tallyhand needs no account and the first 3 invoices or estimates are free with every feature. For a one off, our free invoice generator works in the browser.

What should an invoice for lawn care or cleaning include?

Your business name and contact, the client, the date and due date, each service with its price, the total, and how to pay. See our trade templates for lawn care and cleaning.
