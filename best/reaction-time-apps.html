Reaction time apps and reflex games, compared

To measure one reaction time in milliseconds, use Human Benchmark or our free browser test. To practice under pressure as a game, try BlitzTap. Reaction Time: Test and Training has the most variety, and Reflex suits racing fans.

Updated November 25, 2026 by Graysmith Labs

We make one of these. BlitzTap is our app, listed first so you know which it is. This is a comparison, not a ranking. Details on the others come from their App Store listings and reviews. How we choose apps.

What we looked for

  • Training, not just testing. A test tells you a number; a game you come back to moves it.
  • Fair to play. No energy systems, wait timers or pay to win.
  • Short sessions. Rounds of a minute or two.
  • Progress you can see. Stats, tiers and something to compare with friends.

4 ways to do it, and who each suits

  1. Best for practice as a game · our app

    BlitzTap

    A target shape appears and you tap its match in a grid before the timer runs out. The timer shrinks tier by tier until it is close to one second, which trains speed and visual search together. Play Classic, a 60 second Time Attack, a no timer Zen mode, or the Daily Challenge, where every player gets the same 30 targets. It is free with ads, and a one time purchase removes them.

    • Four modes and a shared Daily Challenge
    • No energy timers and nothing pay to win
    • One time purchase, no subscription

    Pick something else if: It scores rounds, not a single millisecond reading.

    Get BlitzTap on the App Store About BlitzTap

  2. Best quick reaction time test

    Human Benchmark

    The classic browser test: wait for the screen to turn green, then tap. It gives you a millisecond average and a chart of where you sit.

    • Free, in any browser
    • Well known benchmark

    Pick something else if: A test, not a game you will keep playing.

  3. Best for variety

    Reaction Time: Test & Training

    This app bundles dozens of reaction tests and training activities, free with ads, with a one time Pro unlock for all of them and Game Center leaderboards.

    • Many test types
    • One time Pro unlock

    Pick something else if: Feels more like a toolbox than a game.

  4. Best for racing fans

    Reflex: Reaction Time Trainer

    A set of eight small games, including a start lights test inspired by Formula 1, that measure reaction time to the millisecond.

    • F1 style start lights
    • Several game types

    Pick something else if: A newer app with few ratings so far.

About BlitzTap, our app

BlitzTap is a free reaction time game for iPhone and iPad for anyone who wants to test and train their reflexes and hand eye coordination in rounds of a minute or two: a target shape appears and you tap its match in a grid before the timer runs out, in Classic, Time Attack (60 seconds), Zen or a Daily Challenge. It is free with ads, with no energy timers, no wait timers and nothing pay to win; a one time purchase removes ads.

Free with ads. A one time purchase removes ads. No subscription.

Questions

What is a good reaction time?

Most people average around 200 to 300 milliseconds on a simple tap test. Try our free reaction time test to see yours.

Can you improve your reaction time with an app?

Practice helps with the specific skill you practice. A game like BlitzTap trains fast visual search and tapping under time pressure, and short daily sessions such as its Daily Challenge are the easiest way to keep at it.

Is BlitzTap free?

Yes. BlitzTap is free with ads on iPhone and iPad, with no energy timers. A one time purchase removes ads; there is no subscription.
