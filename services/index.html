Fixed price app audit

Find out what is holding your app back.

A short review of your app, codebase and App Store listing, priced up front, with a written report of what to fix first. A good first step before any larger project.

Request an audit
  • App Review risk. The rules that reject builds in practice, such as prices or a paywall in listing screenshots, placeholder text and incomplete subscription details.
  • Subscriptions and paywall. Products, trials and entitlements checked against what the store really offers, and the paywall checked against what the listing promises.
  • Ads and privacy. Ad placement against AdMob policy, the tracking prompt, test ad IDs and the App Privacy answers.
  • Listing and search. Title, subtitle, keywords and screenshots, with copy you can paste in.
  • Code and builds. Dependencies, build setup, crash risks and where AI could save your team time.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Just ask.

What does Graysmith Labs build?

iOS and Android apps with React Native and Expo or native Swift, web apps and sites, backends and serverless APIs, and AI features and automation. It also sets up subscriptions with RevenueCat, payments with Stripe and ads with AdMob, and takes apps through App Store review.

How much does an app cost?

It depends on scope, so there are no list prices. After a free scoping call you get a written plan and a fixed quote for a defined build. Contract engineering is billed at a weekly or monthly rate.

What is the fixed price app audit?

A short review of your app, codebase and App Store listing, priced before it starts. It covers App Review risks, subscriptions and the paywall, ads and privacy, the listing and search, and the code and build setup, and ends with a written report of what to fix first.

Can you take over an existing React Native or Expo app?

Yes. Contract engineering is built for that: we join your repos, tools and review process, and an audit is a good first step to see where the app stands.

Who owns the code?

You do. Once the work is paid for, the code, designs and accounts belong to your company, and everything is documented so any engineer can pick it up.

How do I start?

Email ramsey@graysmithlabs.com with what you are building, who it is for and when you need it. The reply sets up the free scoping call.
