How to write an estimate

  1. Add your business. Your name and how to reach you go at the top, so the client can say yes.
  2. Add the client. Their name and the job address or email go under Prepared for.
  3. Number and date it. Use a new number for every estimate, like EST-0001, and set a valid until date, often 30 days out.
  4. List the work. One line per task or material with a quantity and rate, such as 6 hours of labor at 55 or 4 gallons of stain at 42. The estimated total adds itself up, with tax if you charge it.
  5. Write your terms. A deposit to book, when the rest is due, and that extra work is priced before it starts. Clear terms stop most disputes.
  6. Download, send and follow up. Send the PDF the same day you see the job, and check in after three days if you have not heard back.

Questions

Is this estimate generator really free?

Yes. There is no account, no watermark and no limit on how many estimates you make. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

What is the difference between an estimate and an invoice?

An estimate tells the client what the job should cost before you start, so they can say yes. An invoice asks to be paid once the work is done. When the client accepts the estimate, send an invoice for the same lines when you finish.

Is an estimate the same as a quote?

Close. A quote is usually a fixed price for exactly the work listed, while an estimate is your best figure and can change if the job changes. Either way, list each line of work, the total and how long the price holds. You can title the PDF either way by writing Quote in the notes.

What should an estimate include?

Your business name and contact details, the client's name, an estimate number, the date and a valid until date, each line of work with quantity and rate, the estimated total with any tax, and your terms, such as a deposit to book or what happens if the job grows.

How long should an estimate be valid?

Thirty days is common and is the default here. Use a shorter window, such as 14 days, when material prices move quickly.
